# faa-amt-powerplant audit, part e (topics 9 and 10)

Scope: 09-engine-fuel-and-fuel-metering.json (186 cards) and 10-induction-and-cooling.json (89 cards), 275 cards in all.
Sources: FAA-H-8083-32B (cached text), AC 43.13-1B, the Aviation Mechanic ACS. I checked every number in both topics against the handbook, and they all match: 0.067 and 15:1, 0.0725–0.080, 0.060–0.065, 100 °F, 38 °F, 32 °F, 1/8 inch, 28 "Hg, 10–50 rpm, 8 hours, 1½ times working pressure, 16 inches, 10 and 74 micron, 30 and 40 "Hg, 5,000 ft, 230–300 °F, the factor of five, 108–110 and 115 per cent, 5 minutes, fin limits of 6/4/2/1 inches, and one quarter and one half of the heat.
choicesExplained: on all 69 scenario cards (50 in topic 9, 21 in topic 10), each letter explained is a wrong option and every wrong option is explained. The key is never among them, and each line matches its choice. Topic 10 uses the "X is wrong:" style, which is consistent within that topic.
