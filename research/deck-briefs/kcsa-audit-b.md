# KCSA deck audit B (topics 10 to 18)

Audited 25 September 2026 under `research/deck-briefs/AUDIT.md`. Scope: `decks/kcsa/notes/10-*.json` to `18-*.json`, 504 cards (topic 10: 55, 11: 43, 12: 83, 13: 55, 14: 35, 15: 50, 16: 104, 17: 51, 18: 28). Cards were checked against the saved sources in the scratch folder: the kubernetes/website clone at commit 1e1be1d (docs v1.37), the tag-security whitepaper and lexicon, the Trail of Bits 2019 threat model text, the financial-user-group attack trees, the tool doc clones, the NIST glossary pages, and the saved ATT&CK, CIS, OWASP and SLSA pages. Nothing was fetched except one NIST glossary page, which matched the saved copy. The KCNA deck and KCSA topics 1 to 9 count as known terms.

**Findings: 125 lines.** 15 wrong, 38 unsupported, 6 ambiguous, 12 unclear, 54 minor. The other 379 cards raised nothing.

## The three checks asked for

**Version-dependent behaviour.** Every volatile card that gives a version number matches the saved v1.37 docs: KMS v1 deprecated in 1.28 and v2 stable in 1.29, CVE feed beta in 1.27, signed artifacts beta in 1.26, endPort stable in 1.25, RotateKubeletServerCertificate beta and on by default, CSR expirationSeconds minimum 600 from v1.22. The problems are elsewhere:
- **Wrong on current behaviour:** `expiration-below-minimum` (a CSR below 600 seconds is refused as invalid; it does not fall back to the default), and `flow-unverified-scenario` (the API-server-to-kubelet path is HTTPS today, just with no certificate check; the 2019 model's "plain HTTP" no longer holds).
- **Should be marked volatile or scoped:** `kubelet-authn-authz` and `app-kubelet-alwaysallow-risk` (these are the command-line defaults when no --config file is given), `flow-apiserver-worker` (2019 value), `controlling-nodes-podnodeselector` (beta), `ssh-tunnels-deprecated`, `falco-components` (the docs now list five components), and `attack-classify-persistence-tactic`.
- **Cite pages removed from the docs:** `etcd-encryption-recap`, `cloud-api-least-privilege`, `node-network-acl` and `ingress-tls` cite the 4C's page, which was removed after v1.28. They are marked volatile and cite the release-1.28 copy, which is acceptable.

**Source tiers.** No card in topics 10 to 18 cites the Sigstore docs. Topic 18's signing cards cite the Kubernetes "Verify Signed Kubernetes Artifacts" page, which is tier B. Topic 16 does not cite SLSA; SLSA appears only in topic 17. These cards rest on a tier C source:
- **MITRE ATT&CK (tier C, per brief S26):** `what-is-attack-mitre-containers`, `attack-matrix-shape` and `attack-tactics-current`, plus the live-matrix half of `attack-tactics-whitepaper-name` and `contrast-stride-vs-attack-mitre`. The first two can move to the tier B whitepaper, which states the same facts.
- **SLSA spec (tier C, per brief S24):** `slsa-measures-ssdf`, `ssdf-slsa-scenario`, `what-is-eo-cybersecurity` and `eo-date-affirmed`. `what-is-ssdf`, `what-is-supply-chain-compliance` and `supply-chain-compliance-evidence` are labelled B, but part of what they say rests on SLSA. The EO cards can move to the Executive Order itself (a US federal work, tier B).
- **SPIFFE docs (tier C, per brief S25), mislabelled "B · Apache 2.0":** `spiffe`, `spiffe-svid-short-lived`, `spiffe-vs-ip-identity` and `workload-identity-choice`. The saved LICENSE is a BSD-style "All rights reserved" notice with an Apache text after a stray "=======" marker.
- **CIS and OWASP (tier C, CC BY-NC-SA, labelled correctly):** `what-are-cis-benchmarks` (its wording is close to CIS's own), and five OWASP cards.

**Figure `media/trustboundaries.png`.** It is byte-identical to the saved attack-trees image (md5 634659a8…), and "B · Apache 2.0" is the correct licence: the repo LICENSE is Apache 2.0, with no copyright line or NOTICE file. What needs fixing:
- **Credit:** it lacks the figure's title ("Kubernetes Architecture and Trust Boundaries") and its URL.
- **Licence text:** Apache 2.0 requires shipping the licence text, so check that the deck's licence notice includes it.
- **Card fit:** the figure is on `what-is-data-flow`, which cites Trail of Bits, while the figure comes from the attack trees. It also does not show what the back teaches: it gives no protocol or authentication for any flow, and its trust boundaries circle single components rather than the six Trail of Bits zones.
- **Alt text:** it says the flows are numbered 1 to 16, but the figure also has an unnumbered "*. Read / Write" flow, and "16" appears twice.
- **Out of date:** flow 8 says "Docker run".
- **Diagram not shown:** `zone-image-scenario` refers to "the trust boundaries diagram" but does not display it.

## Most serious

1. `threat-model.zone-crossing-scenario`: the keyed answer says a container must cross two boundaries to reach the API server. Pods reach it directly with their service account token, and the deck's own compromised-container cards teach that path.
2. `isolation-multi-tenancy.namespace-weak-boundary`, with `namespace-two-dimensions` and `workload-creation-namespace-scenario`: these teach that namespace boundaries are weak. The source says boundaries *within* a namespace are weak, and that namespaces should separate trust levels.
3. `pki-certificates.expiration-below-minimum`: the answer (fall back to the default duration) is wrong, and none of the options is right. Also wrong on current behaviour: `cluster-component-security.401-vs-403` (the anonymous-access rule is inverted) and `runtime-socket-hostpath-block` (a read-only mount does not make a runtime-socket mount safe).
