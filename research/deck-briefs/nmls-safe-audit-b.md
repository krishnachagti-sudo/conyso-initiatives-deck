# nmls-safe audit B: topics 04–08

Scope: decks/nmls-safe/notes/04-federal-laws-regulators.json to 08-ethics.json, 493 cards (04: 105, 05: 124, 06: 100, 07: 91, 08: 73; the brief said 433, but the files hold 493). Every card was checked against its `evidence` first. Claims beyond the evidence were checked in research/sources/nmls-safe/. Short fragments from two-column PDFs (16 CFR 681, 31 CFR 1029, 16 CFR 314, 16 CFR 310, 47 CFR 64, 12 CFR 1014, the 2006 nontraditional guidance, the home loan toolkit) were read back into their full passages, and each card was checked against the whole passage. All of them are supported except those listed below. For what each source may be used for, see CONTENT-POLICY.md §3.

**FHA and HECM.** No card in these topics cites hud.gov. FHA premium facts rest on Regulation Z (1026.32 example), the ATR/QM guide (PDF pp. 46–47) and the toolkit (PDF p. 9). HECM facts rest on Regulation X 1024.2. All are supported.

**NMLS pages not in the cache.** The Model State Law (MSL-Final.pdf) and two handbook pages (intro-1, retake) have `path: null` in manifest.json, so their 20 cards carry no evidence. I fetched the three documents once, with a generic User-Agent, to check them, and did not add them to the cache. Every one of those cards is supported: purpose (p. 1), expiry and reinstatement and renewal fees (p. 8), enforcement (p. 10), the $25,000 penalty "for each act or omission" (p. 11), prohibited acts (1), (4), (5), (6), (10) and (11) with the note "Practices 6, 8 and 10 directly support requirements in the S.A.F.E. Act" (p. 15), and MORTGAGE CALL REPORTS and UNIQUE IDENTIFIER SHOWN (p. 16). The handbook says "requiring ALL mortgage loan originators to be either state-licensed or federally registered" and "After every third failed test ... 180-day wait", "separate test enrollment for each attempt". This is tier C (CONTENT-POLICY.md §3), used for facts in our own words. It is still worth caching these files so the checker can confirm the evidence.
