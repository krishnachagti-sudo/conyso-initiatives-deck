# irs-ea-part1 audit B: topics 4 to 7

Scope: 357 cards (04 deductions and credits 175, 05 taxation 58, 06 advising individuals 47, 07 estate, gift and international 77). Every 2025 dollar figure was checked against the cached 2025 sources, including the 2025 legislation changes: standard deduction, the four Schedule 1-A deductions, the $40,000 limit on state and local taxes, CTC $2,200 and ACTC $1,700, the adoption credit, EIC limits, AOTC/LLC, PTC above 400%, AMT, the Social Security wage base, the saver's credit, the QBI thresholds, BEA/GST, the annual exclusion and the FEIE. All of these are correct. Scenario arithmetic was checked step by step; the only errors are listed below. The charitable limits (60%, and 50/30/20%), the carryover and the $250 acknowledgment cards are correct for 2025.

The patch rewrites the scenario with a return filed late on extension (1 June 2026 → 1 June 2032). | "Returns filed before the due date are treated as being filed on the due date."
