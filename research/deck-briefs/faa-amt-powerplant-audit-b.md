# faa-amt-powerplant audit b: topics 2 (turbine engines) and 3 (engine inspection)

353 cards checked: topic 2, 172 cards (51 scenarios); topic 3, 181 cards (15 scenarios). Every scenario's choicesExplained letters match its choices: each wrong letter is explained once and the key is never explained. Every number in topic 2 was compared with the Powerplant handbook (FAA-H-8083-32B). These include 3,500 °F to 1,500 °F, 60 to 70 percent, 80 to 85 percent, about 80 percent, 1.25:1, 8:1, 45°, .8 Mach, 5 minutes, 32.2 ft/sec², 4:1 and 2:1 to 4:1. Every number in topic 3 was compared with the handbook and AC 43.13-1B: 80 psi, 15 to 20 psi, 25 percent, 60/80, 3 minutes, 4 hours and 1 to 2 lb. All PDF page numbers were checked. Every worked calculation was done again. Only thrust-calc-1 has a wrong explanation.

## Checker fixes made directly in the topic files
The checker flagged 36 uses of 4 terms before any primer: topic 2 trend-shift and trend-apply; topic 3 engine-life-limited-part, tbo, ppalt-fuel, proprep-overhaul-cp, overhauled-steps, rebuilt-standard, life-status, temporary-removal, control-aim, all eight method-* cards, transfer, overhaul-periods-manual, ratings-grades, records-time-since-overhaul, sudden-stoppage-def, condition-monitoring, life-factors, faa-mandatory-times, the four stoppage-* cards, cold-cylinder, overhauled-untested, disk-at-limit, flex-mounts-ac, nose-over and new-engine-fines. All four terms are ones the General deck already teaches, and faa-amt-powerplant-terms.json registers them as topic 0. So no primer was added. Each term was removed from those cards' `uses`:
- "overhaul": amtg.regulations.overhaul-vs-rebuild, records-a2-overhaul
- "life-limited parts": amtg.regulations.life-limited-part-def
- "avgas grades and colours": amtg.ground-operations.avgas-grades-in-use and the avgas-*-colour cards
- "special inspections": amtg.inspection.special-also-called and the scenarios that follow it

General teaches these only in fact cards, never in a primer, so the checker cannot see them. The lasting fix is to list them in deck.json `assumedTerms` or give General primers for them. deck.json was not touched. Topics 2 and 3 are now clean in the checker. The deck's 3 remaining problems are in other topics: fuel-metering and two concepts that have no application card.
