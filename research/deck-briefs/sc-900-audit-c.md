# sc-900 audit, part c (topics 9–11: Microsoft Sentinel, Defender XDR, Service Trust Portal and privacy)

Cards checked: 77 + 148 + 41 = 266 (every card in 09-, 10- and 11-). Tier C pages re-read on 2026-09-26: the SIEM/SOAR, Sentinel and Security Copilot units; the Defender XDR units 2–7; the Defender Vulnerability Management and Defender threat intelligence docs; the Service Trust Portal unit and get-started guide; the privacy principles unit; the SC-900 study guide. Volatile facts confirmed as current: 350+ connectors, Azure portal retirement 31 March 2027, data lake up to 12 years, advanced hunting up to 30 days, Vulnerability Management under Exposure management, the four STP restricted-document roles, the NDA for Compliance Materials, Defender for Office 365 and Defender for Endpoint in Plan 1 and Plan 2.

Systemic: 36 scenario cards here (and about 90 more in topics 1–8 and 12–14, by the same test) have a choicesExplained that calls the keyed right answer wrong and never explains one distractor. Cause: build/spread-answers.mjs swapLabels only swaps a letter followed by ) : , ; . or “and/or X”, so “B is wrong: …” and “B covers …” kept their old letters when the answer moved.
