# ama-pcm audit, part b (topics 5 to 9)

168 cards checked (05-b2b-buyer-behaviour to 09-product-life-cycle-and-new-product-development).
Patches: `ama-pcm-fixes-b.jsonl` (19 value lines, 16 needs-research lines); the dry run is clean.

Checks the assignment asked for, with nothing to flag:
- **Scenario letters.** In all 35 scenario cards, the back matches its choice and the choicesExplained letters are exactly the three wrong options. No letter inside a word was changed: every stand-alone capital "A" left in the text is the article "A".
- **Target market's "five basic strategies".** The list names four: undifferentiated or mass, differentiated, concentrated or niche, and micromarketing (Target market, "Target market strategies"). No card says "five", and the four strategy cards match the four in the list.
- **Market segmentation's "stable".** No card teaches stability as a criterion. The criterion cards (identifiability, substantiality, accessibility, responsiveness, actionable) match the five definitions the article gives. The one exception is the identifiability explanation, flagged below.
- **Straight rebuy.** The back names only the Buygrid model (Robinson et al., 1967) and its three buy classes. The article defines no class except new task. The example went further and is flagged.
- **AMA.** No card in T5 to T9 states an exam fact, a price, or an AMA mark used as a noun.
- **OpenStax.** Nothing appears to come from OpenStax. The only material that reads like a textbook and is not in the cited article is the straight-rebuy example ("regular" buys), flagged below.
- **Other banners, not flagged.** Product line extension carries "may contain original research" (June 2024) and "needs more citations". No card rests on a sentence marked "citation needed", "disputed" or "clarification needed".
