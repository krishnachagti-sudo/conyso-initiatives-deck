# Audit B: fema-ics, topics 11–20 (IS-700.b, IS-800.d)

Audited 25 September 2026 against the saved sources in the scratch folder `sources/fema-ics/`: SMIS0700b, NIMS2017, NIMS2017_ISLM, NRF2019, NPG2015 and lifelines. www.fema.gov was not fetched. The `image` fields were skipped, and so were the mechanical rules the checker enforces.

**Scope:** 471 cards in 10 topic files, 11 to 20 (33 + 44 + 32 + 39 + 28 + 47 + 45 + 69 + 36 + 98).
**Result:** 197 problem lines covering 170 distinct cards: wrong 5, unsupported 11, ambiguous 25, unclear 1, minor 155. 81 of the minor lines are page-link errors only (#page= off by one visual, or the NRF's printed page used instead of the PDF page).

## The specific checks asked for

- **Community lifelines: correct.** Every lifeline card teaches the **seven** of NRF 2019 p14: Safety and Security; Food, Water, Shelter; Health and Medical; Energy (Power & Fuel); Communications; Transportation; Hazardous Material. Only one card mentions the eighth. `fema.lifelines.eight-now` is extra and volatile, and it matches the saved lifelines page ("Last updated March 8, 2024"), which adds "Water Systems" and "Food, Hydration, Shelter". No other card leaks the eight-lifeline version.
- **The 15 ESFs and their coordinators: correct.** All 15 names and all 15 coordinators match NRF Table 4 (PDF pp45–47), including ESF #2 and ESF #14 = DHS/CISA, ESF #4 = USDA/Forest Service with DHS/FEMA/USFA, ESF #7 = GSA with DHS/FEMA, ESF #13 = DOJ/ATF and ESF #15 = DHS. ESF #14 is described correctly as new in 2019 and as superseding the CIKR and Private Sector support annexes. Two problems remain. First, `esf-coordinator` claims "exactly one coordinator per ESF", which contradicts #4 and #7. Second, the ESF #1–#5 name cards link to PDF p39 instead of p45.
- **NIMS 2017 wording: correct, with three exceptions.** The deck gets these right: three components; three guiding principles, with Unity of Effort new in 2017; MACS as the umbrella for ICS, EOCs, MAC Groups and JIS; the four communications principles, with Security new; the three EOC structures; activation levels 3/2/1 (Level 1 = Full Activation); the four ISM sections; and the MAC Group not performing command. The three errors are: the 2008 component list (`components-2008`, wrong); "incident objectives" relabelled as "Operations Section objectives"; and the unrequested-resources "deplete" item, whose meaning is changed.
- **§7 traps: none found.** No card uses "Center Management System", teaches "Command and Management" as a 2017 component, teaches 3–7 span of control, makes I/I a fixed sixth section, cites older course letters, or states a question count or time limit.
- **NRF 2019 wording: mostly correct.** The five guiding principles, the tiered response phrase, the declarations, the LFA, the Stafford Act request rules, the NOC/NRCC/RRCC/JOC/SIOC and the UCG/JFO all check out. The main error is `app-catastrophic-threshold`: it treats a hurricane that overwhelms one state as a *national* catastrophic incident.

## Three most serious problems
1. `fema.nrf.app-catastrophic-threshold` gives the wrong answer. A state plus its neighbours being overwhelmed does not meet the NRF's national test ("the Nation's collective capability … would be overwhelmed").
2. `fema.nims-fundamentals.components-2008` names the 2008 components wrongly. It leaves out "Ongoing Management and Maintenance" and invents a split of "Command and Management from Coordination structures".
3. `fema.esf.esf-coordinator` says "exactly one coordinator per ESF", which contradicts Table 4 for ESF #4 and #7. The other two "wrong" lines also change what the source means: `unrequested-deplete` (NIMS says self-deployers drain their *home community's* resources) and `eoc-forms`.
