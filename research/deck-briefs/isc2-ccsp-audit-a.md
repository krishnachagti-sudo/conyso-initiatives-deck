# isc2-ccsp audit A: topics 01–06

Scope: decks/isc2-ccsp/notes/01-concepts-roles.json to 06-cloud-data-protection.json, 336 cards. I checked every card against its `evidence` first. Claims that go beyond the evidence were checked in research/sources/isc2-ccsp/ (SP 500-292, 800-41r1, 800-53r5, 800-57pt1r5, 800-88r2, 800-125, 800-144, 800-190, 800-207, 800-209, 800-218, AI 100-1, AI 100-2e2025, IR 8053).

The manifest keeps no text for tier C sources. I fetched three of them once, to scratch space only, with a generic User-Agent and no personal details: ITU-T Y.3500, ITU-T Y.3502 and the ENISA 2009 cloud risk assessment. I checked every ITU and ENISA card against them for facts only (CONTENT-POLICY.md §3, tier C). They support the cards, apart from the findings below.

**Primers that rest on a term's use in a NIST source.** SOAR, DevSecOps, zero trust, hardening, immutable infrastructure, serverless, ephemeral computing and ephemeral storage were checked. Three claim more than their source: serverless, ephemeral computing and ephemeral storage. Hardening is patched to show how CM-6 links to the term. SOAR and DevSecOps claim no more than the source (the DevSecOps front says the meaning is the deck's own). "Immutable infrastructure" is not SP 800-190's phrase, but the back is supported on p. 25, so I left it.
