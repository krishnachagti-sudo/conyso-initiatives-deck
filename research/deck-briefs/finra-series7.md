## Traps

- **Pattern day trader rules are gone from Rule 4210.** Effective June 4, 2026 (SR-FINRA-2025-017), 4210 defines "intraday margin level" and "intraday margin deficit". A search of the current text finds no "pattern day trader" and no $25,000 minimum. Deficits stay outstanding until "the fifteenth business day". The © 2025 outline and most prep material still say "pattern day trading". Teach the new rule and flag the old one as outdated `[T14]` `[T3]`.
- **Rule 504 limit.** The outline heading still reads "Exemption for Limited Offerings and Sales of Securities Not Exceeding $5,000,000". The current CFR text reads "$10,000,000" `[T2]`.
- **Commercial paper exemption** is stated in the statute as "not exceeding nine months" (Securities Act §3(a)(3)). Cards should quote that, not a converted day count, unless another source is added `[T10]`.
- **Settlement is T+1.** Rule 15c6-1 bars settlement "later than the first business day after the date of the contract" (current text 2023-05-05). Material written before then still teaches T+2 `[T13]`.
- **IRA numbers are year-specific.** Pub 590-A (2025): "the IRA contribution limit remains $7,000 ($8,000 for individuals age 50 or older)". Form 709 instructions: "The annual gift exclusion for 2025 is $19,000." Cards must carry `validAsOf: 2025` `[T3]` `[T6]`.
- **Rollover limit is one per 12-month period, not per calendar year.** Pub 590-A: "one rollover in any 12-month period" `[T3]`.
- **"No-load" label.** Rule 2341 bars the label if 12b-1-type charges "exceed .25 of 1% of average net assets per annum", even with no front-end or deferred load `[T8]`.
- **Maximum sales charge.** 2341: without an asset-based charge, the charge "shall not exceed 8.5% of the offering price" `[T8]`.
- **Options account agreement.** 2360: the background information goes to the customer for verification "within fifteen (15) days after the customer's account has been approved for options trading". This is different from the ODD delivery requirement `[T4]` `[T9]`.
- **Series 7 municipal authority** changed for registrations on or after 7 Nov 2011: sales to and purchases from customers only `[T11]`.
- **Section 1256 60/40** covers "any nonequity option" (and dealer equity options), not ordinary listed equity options: 26 U.S.C. 1256 gives "60 percent" long-term treatment `[T9]`.
- **Retake waits** are calendar days (15 / 60), counted from the last attempt `[exam facts]`.
- **Records.** Rule 17a-4 retention is "not less than 6 years" (first two years easily accessible) or 3 years depending on the record. Complaint records under FINRA 4513 are kept "at least four years" `[T12]` `[T13]`.
- **ACATS.** Validate within "one business day", complete within "three business days" after validation (11870) `[T12]`.
- **Market-wide circuit breakers** are defined in 6121.02 as Level 1, 2 and 3 Market Declines. The percentages were not confirmed in the fetched text, so leave them off the cards until verified `[T13]`.
