# finra-sie audit, part b (topics 4–6)

289 cards checked: 04 packaged products (137), 05 investment risks (34), 06 trading, returns and settlement (118). Sources: cached text in `research/sources/finra-sie/`, plus 26 U.S.C. 529 read once from uscode.house.gov (not cached). No finra.org or msrb.org page was used.

Settlement: T+1 is taught correctly throughout (t-plus-one, tuesday-trade, friday-trade, trade-date, t1-exclusions, late-priced-offering, all matching Rule 15c6-1(a) and (c)). No card in these topics teaches T+2, and the ex-dividend cards use the current "record date, or one business day before" rule.

Named terms: "capital risk", "reinvestment risk" and "political risk" appear in no cached source. The cards are now worded so that they claim only what the SEC pages say and do not imply that the SEC uses these names.
