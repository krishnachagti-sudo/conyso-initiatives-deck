# fcc-extra audit A: topics 01–07

Scope: decks/fcc-extra/notes/01- to 07-*.json, 292 cards (121 pool cards, 171 primer, fact, contrast, classification and application cards).
For pool cards I checked only the explanation and choicesExplained, and whether the explanation really says why the key is right.
Calculations: I reworked every numerical card step by step. These are all correct: E5A02, E5A10, E5A11, E5A12, E5B04, E5B07, E5B08, E5B11, E5C10, E5C11, E5C12, E5D11, bandwidth-calc, time-constant-calc, phase-angle-calc, net-reactance-scenario, real-power-scenario, jumper-length-scenario, beta-scenario, beta-primer example, half-power example, solar-cell-count, battery-time-apply, regulator-dissipation-apply and dropout-apply. Every distractor derivation is also correct, except the E5A10 line below.
Figures: I checked media/figure-e5-1.png, figure-e6-1.png, figure-e6-2.png and figure-e7-2.png against the pool figures and the alt texts. The point coordinates in E5-1, the JFET arrow directions in E6-1 (1 points out, 6 points in), the hooked bar on symbol 6 in E6-2 and the E7-2 wiring (R1 and C2 at Q1's base, D1 to ground, C3 0.01 and R2 across the output) all match. Credit and licence are correct on every card that uses them.
