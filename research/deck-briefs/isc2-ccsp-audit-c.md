# isc2-ccsp audit C: topics 13–17

Scope: decks/isc2-ccsp/notes/13-identity-access.json to 17-audit-risk-contracts.json, 280 cards (29 + 57 + 60 + 62 + 72). I checked each card against its `evidence` first. Then I grepped research/sources/isc2-ccsp/ for claims the evidence does not cover (SP 800-53r5, 800-61r3, 800-63-4, 800-39, 800-94, 800-125, 800-122, NISTIR 8006).

Tier C cards (ITU-T Y.3500, the DPDP Act 2023, PIPEDA, AICPA SOC page, ENISA 2009) were checked for facts only. Under CONTENT-POLICY.md §3 they must be in our own words, and none of them quotes its source. The GDPR cards were checked against the cached text in research/sources/iapp-cippe/. Y.3500, the DPDP Act, PIPEDA, the AICPA page and ENISA 2009 had no cached text. I fetched each once, with a generic User-Agent and no personal detail, into scratch only. The meity.gov.in PDF refused the first request and was served on the second.

All five check out. DPDP ss. 2(g), 2(i)–(k), 8(1), 8(2), 8(6) and 16 are on the cited PDF pages 2, 7 and 11. PIPEDA ss. 4(1) and 10.1 match. AICPA says CPAs provide the SOC suite, and its SOC 2 guide title names the five categories. ENISA p. 22 matches, and it bases its estimates on ISO/IEC 27005:2008. Y.3500 3.1.7 is on p. 8 and the cloud SLA is on p. 14.

Not reported: ssae-16-example cites SSAE 16 as the guide does (SSAE 18 has since replaced it). It is a minor finding with no source in the set for a patch.
