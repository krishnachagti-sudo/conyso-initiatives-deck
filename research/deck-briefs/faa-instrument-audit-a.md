# faa-instrument audit, part A (topics 1–5, 269 cards)

Checked against the cached 14 CFR 61.3, 61.51, 61.57, 61.65, 91.109, 91.167, 91.169, 91.171, 91.173, 91.177, 91.179, 91.205, 91.213, 91.411, 91.413; AIM 1-1, 5-1, 5-2, 7-1; IFH (FAA-H-8083-15B); IPH (FAA-H-8083-16B); Aviation Weather Handbook (FAA-H-8083-28B); Instrument Rating ACS (FAA-S-ACS-8C); Testing Matrix (25 September 2026).

Currency (61.57(c), (d)), alternate (91.169(b), (c)) and fuel (91.167) cards match the current rule text. The knowledge-test card gives 2.0 hours, which agrees with the Testing Matrix. No card in topics 1–5 teaches a retired weather product: weather-icing.gfa-replaced-fa names the area forecast only as the legacy product the GFA replaced. Cloze cards with no `back` and pool backs written "B: …" rather than "B) …" are left to the checker.
