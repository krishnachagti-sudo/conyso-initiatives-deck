## Checks with no finding
- Every scenario card's answer letter matches its choice, and every choicesExplained covers exactly the other letters (script check over all 40 scenario cards). No letter inside a word was altered: "R&D", "4Ps", "4Cs" and the quotation "‘A world…’" are intact.
- No card states exam facts, prices or AMA/PCM marks as nouns. "AMA" appears only inside quoted Wikipedia evidence, and cards say "American Marketing Association".
- None of the cited articles carries "disputed" or a machine-generated-text notice. Other "citation needed" tags (Marketing mix: convenience and communications; Porter's five forces: the Grove sixth-force claim; Maslow: universality) sit next to passages the cards use, but no card rests on the tagged text.
- Nothing reads as drawn from OpenStax. Every claim traced to the cited Wikipedia section.
