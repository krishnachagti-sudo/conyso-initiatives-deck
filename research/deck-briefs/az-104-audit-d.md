DNS primers: the zone primer rests on the tutorial and the record-set primer on the TTL text, as the brief warned. The zone primer tied its records to delegation, and the record-set primer claimed more than the TTL passage says. Both are patched to claim only what those passages support.

az104.dns-load-balancing.ts-icmp | wrong | Says a public load balancer's frontend gives no ping reply because rules carry only TCP/UDP. A Standard public frontend answers ping itself while a backend is healthy. The ICMP limit applies to internal load balancers without HA ports. Front, back and explanation rewritten for the internal case. | "Standard Public Azure Load Balancer frontend IPv4 and IPv6 addresses support testing reachability using ping"; "At least 1 backend instance is probed UP: Successful echo replies"; "ICMP pings can't be disabled and are allowed by default on Standard Public Load Balancers" (load-balancer/load-balancer-test-frontend-reachability.md, "Expected replies with ping"); "except that an internal Standard Load Balancer supports ICMP traffic when you enable HA ports" (components.md, Limitations)
