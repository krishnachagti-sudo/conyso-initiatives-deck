## Naming

- NMLS calls the exam the "SAFE Mortgage Loan Originator Test", the "SAFE MLO
  Test" and the "SAFE MLO National Test with Uniform State Test"
  `[mlo_testing_hbk_intro-1.txt]`, `[mlo_testing_hbk_testOutline.txt]`.
- **Trademarks and terms.** The NMLS Site Terms of Use: "All registered and/or
  unregistered trademarks and/or service marks (collectively, "Marks") used or
  referred to on this website are the property of CSBS, unless otherwise
  noted. You may not use, copy, reproduce, republish, upload, post, transmit,
  distribute or modify these Marks in any way without CSBS's prior written
  permission." On content: "All materials contained on this website are
  copyrighted except where explicitly noted otherwise. All rights reserved."
  and "You may not copy, reproduce, distribute or create derivative works from
  this Content … without CSBS's prior, written consent." `[terms.html]`. I
  found no list of which words CSBS claims as marks.
- **CFPB marks.** "The Bureau's logo, as well as the names Consumer Financial
  Protection Bureau and CFPB are registered trademarks." `[cfpb/legal.txt]`.
  Naming the CFPB as the author of a cited regulation is a true factual
  statement; no logo.
- **Candidate agreement.** "The SAFE MLO Test is a confidential, copyrighted
  test"; candidates agree not to "reconstruct any part of the exam from
  memory". `[mlo_test_hbk_candidateAgreement-1.txt]`. This is tier D: never a
  source. The handbook's Appendix B sample questions were not fetched and must
  not be used.
- **How CONTENT-POLICY.md §4 applies.** CSBS/NMLS is not in §5, so the deck
  may name the exam in plain text, our brand first, to state a true fact.
  Because CSBS's terms are strict (marks "in any way" need permission; no
  derivative works from site content), the outline and handbook are **tier C:
  facts only, in our own words, linked, never copied**, and §2.3 applies: do
  not reproduce the outline on the deck page. No NMLS or CSBS logo, no
  "official", no "NMLS-approved" (NMLS approves education courses, so that
  phrase would be read as a claim). Put §8's lawyer review of the naming ahead
  of launch for this deck in particular. Title, for example: "[Site name] deck
  for the SAFE Mortgage Loan Originator Test (national component)".

## Traps

- `[T5]` **Two retest rules that read differently.** The statute says wait
  "at least 6 months" after 3 consecutive failures (12 U.S.C. 5104(d)(3)(C));
  NMLS applies "one hundred eighty (180) calendar days" and 30 calendar days
  between attempts. Teach the NMLS figures for the test, citing both.
- `[T5]` **Pre-licensing versus continuing education.** 20 hours (3 federal
  law, 3 ethics, 2 nontraditional) before licensing (5104(c)); 8 hours
  (3 federal law, 2 ethics, 2 nontraditional) every year (5105(b),
  12 CFR 1008.107(a)(2)). Candidates swap the ethics hours.
- `[T5]` **Felony bar has two limbs:** any felony in the 7 years before
  application, or a felony "at any time" involving "fraud, dishonesty, a breach
  of trust, or money laundering" (12 CFR 1008.105(b)(1)).
- `[T5]` **Test expiry excludes registered time.** Five years without a valid
  licence, "not taking into account any time during which such individual is
  a registered loan originator".
- `[T5]` **Temporary authority ends at the earliest** of withdrawal, denial or
  notice of intent to deny, grant, or 120 days if the application is listed as
  incomplete (12 U.S.C. 5117(b)(2)).
- `[T5]` **Depository employees register; they do not take the test.** "you
  are not required to take the National Test. As a registrant you are eligible
  to take the National Test." `[mlo_testing_hbk_FAQ.txt]`.
- `[T5]` **The FAQ is out of date on delivery.** It still says the test "is
  available at all Prometric test centers and via Online Proctored Testing";
  the handbook limits online proctoring to ADA accommodations from
  1 October 2024.
- `[T2]` **HPML thresholds differ by lien:** 1.5 points over APOR (first lien
  within the conforming limit), 2.5 (first lien above it), 3.5 (subordinate
  lien) (12 CFR 1026.35(a)(1)). Do not confuse with the HOEPA tests in
  1026.32(a)(1).
- `[T2]` **Rescission runs to midnight of the third business day** after the
  last of consummation, the notice or the material disclosures
  (1026.23(a)(3)(i)); purchase-money loans have no right to rescind
  (1026.23(f)).
- `[T2]`/`[T3]` **Two definitions of business day** in 1026.2(a)(6). The
  general one (offices open for substantially all business) governs Loan
  Estimate delivery; the precise one (all calendar days except Sundays and
  federal legal holidays) governs rescission, the 7-day Loan Estimate wait and
  the Closing Disclosure wait.
- `[T3]` **Loan Estimate by the third business day after application; Closing
  Disclosure received at least three business days before consummation.**
  No revised Loan Estimate once the Closing Disclosure is provided
  (1026.19(e)(4)(ii)).
- `[T3]` **TRID does not cover reverse mortgages** ("other than a reverse
  mortgage subject to § 1026.33", 1026.19(e)(1)(i)). The outline still says
  "HUD-1"; no source read says where the HUD-1 still applies, so do not teach
  it as the normal closing form.
- `[T3]` **Tolerance refunds within 60 days of consummation**
  (1026.19(f)(2)(v)); estimated closing costs expire if intent to proceed comes
  more than 10 business days after delivery (1026.19(e)(3)(iv)(E)).
- `[T1]` **Several 3-business-day deadlines** after application: special
  information booklet (1024.6(a)(1), 1026.19(g)(1)(i)), homeownership
  counselling list (1024.20(a)(1)), Loan Estimate. ECOA's action-taken notice
  is 30 days (1002.9(a)(1)(i)); records kept 25 months (1002.12(b)(1)).
- `[T1]` **Escrow cushion** is at most one-sixth of estimated annual escrow
  payments (1024.17(c)(1)(i)).
- `[T1]` **Foreclosure:** no first notice or filing until the loan is "more
  than 120 days delinquent" (1024.41(f)(1)(i)).
- `[T4]` **PMI:** borrower may ask to cancel at 80% of original value;
  automatic termination at 78% (12 U.S.C. 4902).
- `[T4]` **Reg N records** are kept for twenty-four months (12 CFR 1014,
  GovInfo 1-1-26 edition). SAR threshold for loan or finance companies is
  $5,000 (31 CFR 1029.320).
- `[T6]` **"Yield spread premium"** is still in the outline, but no source read
  uses it; the CFPB's consumer page speaks of "lender credits and points". Teach
  the term only once a source defines it. The CFPB ATR/QM guide was last revised April 2021 for the
  General QM final rule; older prep material may predate it.
