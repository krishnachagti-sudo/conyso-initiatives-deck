## Naming

- **Marks, from the owner's own list.** ADM's trademark list
  (https://www.scrum.org/advanced-development-methods-inc-trademark-list
  `[tmlist.md]`) shows the word mark "PSPO" among the "COURSE LOGOS AND MARKS",
  says "PSPO I badge registered in China", and lists "SCRUM.ORG" and
  "PROFESSIONAL SCRUM" ("Registered in the U.S., U.K. and E.U."). The PSPO I
  page writes the name "Professional Scrum Product Owner™ I". The Nexus Guide
  writes "Nexus™". Symbol rule: "Where a mark is registered in the
  jurisdiction where it is being used the ® symbol should be used. Where the
  mark is pending in the relevant jurisdiction or used in the U.S., a “TM” or
  “SM” should be used."
- **Usage terms** (https://www.scrum.org/scrumorg-trademarks-and-copyrights
  `[tm.md]`):
  - Word marks, but not logos, "may be used in an informational context to
    describe the subject matter of an educational or informational program,
    such as … preparation for ADM certification exams". The mark must appear
    "less prominently than the rest of the program title" and "only in a
    referential context". The guidelines' own examples: "Correct: This
    training is intended to help you prepare for the Scrum.org™ PSM™ I
    assessment." and "Incorrect: This PSM I training…".
  - "Always use the trademark as an adjective, NEVER as a noun or verb."
    "Do NOT make any trademark into a plural or possessive." "Do not
    abbreviate", except for "official abbreviations found on the Trademark
    List". Marks "should … stand out in text in bold or italics,
    particularly if a trademark symbol cannot be used."
  - No logos or badges: "UNLESS EXPRESSLY AUTHORIZED BY ADM, ANY USE OF ADM
    LOGOS AND CERTIFICATION MARKS IS PROHIBITED."
  - No marks in domain names or social handles, and none in "misleading meta

## Traps

- [T1] The Product Owner is "one person, not a committee". They "may delegate" Product Backlog work but "remains accountable".
- [T1] "Only the Product Owner has the authority to cancel the Sprint", and the stated reason is that "the Sprint Goal becomes obsolete".
- [T1] The Product Owner goes to the Daily Scrum only when "actively working on items in the Sprint Backlog", and then "participate[s] as Developers".
- [T1] Stakeholders change the Product Backlog "by trying to convince the Product Owner", not by editing it.
- [T1] Several Scrum Teams on one product "should share the same Product Goal, Product Backlog, and Product Owner".
- [T2] The Product Owner orders. "The Developers who will be doing the work are responsible for the sizing". The PO only helps them "understand and select trade-offs".
- [T2] The Scrum Guide says "ordered" and "Ordering", so cards should use that word and not "prioritized".
- [T2] One Product Goal at a time: "fulfill (or abandon) one objective before taking on the next". The Product Goal is "in the Product Backlog". It is not a separate artifact.
- [T2] Undone items are not shown at the Sprint Review. They "return[] to the Product Backlog".
- [T3] Outputs are not outcomes. More hours and more features "does not necessarily lead to improved customer experiences".
- [T3] Market value KVAs (CV, UV) "reflect customer outcomes". The capability KVAs (A2I, T2M) "may be measured in terms of either outcomes or outputs". This split is the 2024 wording.
- [T3] "EBM defines no specific Key Value Measures". The appendix measures are examples, not requirements.
- [T3] Version drift: the EBM page says a new EBM Guide came out "On May 7, 2024 … in English", and "The prior version will remain available in other languages until they are replaced". Translated EBM guides may be the older version.
- [T3] The 2024 EBM appendix still says "Development Team" (Production Incident Count). The Scrum Guide 2020 term is Developers.
- [T4] Mission Statement is why the organization exists today. Vision Statement is its long-term aspiration (Scrum.org Business Strategy page).
- [T4] The Sprint Review is "a working session", not a presentation. It "should never be considered a gate to releasing value".
- [T5] A forecast "is by no means a commitment or guarantee". Burn-downs and similar "do not replace the importance of empiricism".
- [T5] Releasing can happen at any time in the Sprint ("an Increment may be delivered to stakeholders prior to the end of the Sprint"), but only Done work can be released.
- [T6] The Sprint Goal is the Developers' commitment. Scope is renegotiated with the PO "without affecting the Sprint Goal".
- [T6] Self-managing means the team decides "who does what, when, and how".
- [T0] Scrum.org and scrumguides.org are different things. The Scrum Guide's copyright is the authors', and Scrum.org does not own it.

## Languages

The PSPO I page lists the exam languages as "English", "日本語 (Japanese)" and
"简体中文 (Simplified Chinese) available through scrum.org.cn". It also allows
the Google Translate plugin: "Many people successfully use the Google
Translate Plugin to take the test in their native language", provided
candidates "MUST follow these instructions". Those instructions were not
opened. The 2024 EBM Guide was released in English. Web search results showed
2024 Polish and Dutch EBM PDFs, and Spanish Nexus 2021 PDFs, on Scrum.org's
S3 bucket, but none were fetched or checked. Scrum Guide translations were
not checked in this task.

  base name probably sits in images the reader did not render. The symbol
