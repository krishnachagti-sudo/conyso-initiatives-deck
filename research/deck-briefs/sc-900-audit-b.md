# sc-900 audit B: topics 05–08

Scope: decks/sc-900/notes/05-entra-access-management.json to 08-azure-security-management.json. That is 326 cards (64 + 79 + 116 + 67). None are pool cards. Every card with `evidence` was checked against it first; claims beyond it were grepped in research/sources/sc-900/ (Conditional Access overview, Entra RBAC overview, ID Governance, entitlement management, access reviews, PIM, ID Protection, DDoS Protection, Azure Firewall, WAF, NSGs, ASGs, Bastion, Key Vault, encryption overview). All of those are tier B (CONTENT-POLICY.md §3, tier B).

Cards that cite Microsoft Learn training units (tier C, CONTENT-POLICY.md §3, tier C) were checked against the 17 units themselves, fetched 2026-09-26 into scratch (not cached: no card quotes them). All their claims held, including the volatile licence facts: Conditional Access needs P1 or P2; custom roles need P1 per user (Learn: “P1 or P2”); risk-based policies need P2; foundational CSPM is free and on by default; DSPM comes with Defender CSPM or Defender for Storage.

**Systemic fault (35 of the 42 scenario cards in these topics):** after `build/spread-answers.mjs` moved the right answer, the sentence in `choicesExplained` that explains the option now in the old slot still carries the old letter, so each card calls its keyed answer “wrong” and leaves one distractor unexplained. The script’s `swapLabels` pattern matches “A:”, “A)” and “A and B” but not “A is wrong”. Every such card is patched below with its labels corrected and sorted; the script itself also needs fixing, and topics 01–04 and 09–14 are likely affected too.
