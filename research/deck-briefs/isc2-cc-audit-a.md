# isc2-cc audit, part A (topics 1–10)

Scope: decks/isc2-cc/notes/01-core-security-concepts.json to 10-access-control.json, 367 cards (33, 54, 55, 19, 29, 47, 41, 24, 31, 34). I checked each card against its `evidence` first, then checked any claim that went beyond the evidence in research/sources/isc2-cc/ (FIPS 199; SP 800-12r1, 800-30r1, 800-34r1, 800-37r2, 800-39, 800-50r1, 800-53r5, 800-53B, 800-55v1, 800-63-4, 800-63B-4, 800-82r3, 800-122, 800-137, 800-153, 800-160v1r1, 800-162, 800-207; NISTIR 7316; CSWP 29; 45 CFR 164.304; the GDPR). Every scenario card's choicesExplained names exactly the wrong letters of its own choices.

Tier C: the manifest keeps no text for isc2.org/ethics, so I fetched the page once to scratch space only, with a generic User-Agent and no personal detail. Every fact on the ethics cards matches the page. Under CONTENT-POLICY.md §3 (tier C, "Facts in our own words, linked to the page, never copied"), I reworded the cards that followed the Code's wording closely, including the four canons. No card in topics 1–10 cites the ISC2 exam outline.
