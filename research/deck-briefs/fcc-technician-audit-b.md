# fcc-technician audit, part B (topics 07–12)

Scope: `decks/fcc-technician/notes/07-operating-practice.json` to `12-test-instruments.json`, 300 cards read in `order` (7005–12230). For pass 2, the newcomer is assumed to have studied topics 01–06 (their `introduces` terms were listed from the JSON).
Audited 2026-09-25 against `research/deck-briefs/fcc-technician-pool.txt`, the saved `part97.txt` (eCFR 2026-09-22) and the figure JPGs.

## Mechanical checks (all passed unless listed below)
- **Pool cards (145):** every card whose id ends in a pool ID (T2A01–T2C12, T4A01–T4B11, T5C08–T5C11, T5D01–T5D14, T6A01–T6D11, T7A01–T7A11, T7D02–T7D11) was checked by script. Its `front` matches the pool stem exactly, its `choices` match the pool's four answers exactly, its `back` is the keyed answer and its `source` names the ID. The only differences are three backs that add words after the key (see below). No group in scope is missing a question: T5C01–07, T5C12 and T7D01 are in topic 01.
- **Distractor arithmetic** on T5C09–T5D12 was recomputed, and every "sum/difference/product/upside down" claim holds, except T5C08 D (see below).
- **Figures T-1, T-2, T-3:** each figure matches the pool's numbering, and the keyed answers for T6A09, T6C02–T6C11 and T6D10 match what is drawn. The credit and licence ("A · public domain") match brief §2 S3. The 11 symbol crops each show the right component.
- **Rule quotes:** the text of 97.101(b), 97.101(c), 97.103(a), 97.119(b)(2), 97.3(a)(8), 97.3(a)(38), 97.403 and 97.407(a) was checked against part97.txt. Paraphrases are faithful except where listed.
- **Deck-drawn SVGs:** repeater-offset, ohms-law, series-parallel, station-power-meter and meter-connections were read as source. Their labels and geometry match the cards: the voltmeter is drawn across the component and the ammeter in the loop.
- **Wikipedia:** only one card in scope cites it (`what-is-swr`, below).
