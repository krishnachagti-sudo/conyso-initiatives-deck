## 0. Headline findings for the deck owner

1. **The test changes on 26 October 2026.** The UAG test "will include image-based
   questions not found in the test supplements", with images "from the latest Sectional
   Charts" (ATCA September 2026 Special Edition, p.6,
   https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/September_2026_Special_Edition.pdf). The
   April and June 2026 editions said 27 October; September says "*Corrected date from
   the previous edition". Chart-reading cards should therefore teach the **current
   Chart User's Guide symbols on current sectional excerpts**, not only the 2018 AKTS
   figures.
2. **Two different official weightings exist.** The ACS (2021) gives ranges (Regulations
   15–25%, Operations 35–45%, ...). The PSI CIB effective 29 September 2025 gives
   Regulations 48%, Airspace 20%, Weather 5%, Loading & Performance 2%, Operations 25%,
   "determined by the industry-wide job task analysis study completed in 2025". The CIB
   is the newer statement. Core/extra tagging should follow the CIB weights.
3. **The regulation has moved since the study materials were written.** §107.5
   (falsification) is "[Reserved]" since 3 November 2025 (moved to 14 CFR part 3,
   subpart D); §107.9 is now titled "Safety event reporting" (2022). AC 107-2A (2021)
   and the SG (2016) still describe the old wording. Cards cite eCFR, not the AC, for
   rule text.
4. **Licence is clean:** all core sources are US federal works (17 U.S.C. 105, tier B).
   The only non-federal document is the PSI CIB (© PSI, tier C, facts only).

## 3. Exam outline (FAA-S-ACS-10B, owner's order)

5 Areas of Operation, 17 Tasks, 176 element codes (131 knowledge elements + 45
sub-elements). Every Task's "Risk Management" is "[Reserved]" and "Skills" is "[Not
Applicable]". The ACS is public domain; below is its wording.

**I. Regulations** (ACS 15–25%; CIB 48%)
- **I.A General** (parts 89, 107 subpart A): K1 Applicability of part 107; K2 Definitions; K3 Falsification, reproduction, alteration; K4 Accident reporting; K5 Inspection, testing, demonstration of compliance; K6 Multiple category sUAS; K7 Record retention; K8 Previously manufactured sUAS.
- **I.B Operating Rules** (parts 47, 48, 89, 107 subpart B): K1 Registration; K2 Condition for safe operation; K3 Medical conditions; K4 Responsibility/authority of remote PIC (K4a other person manipulating controls); K5 Deviation and reporting for in-flight emergencies; K6 Hazardous operations (K6a careless or reckless; K6b dropping an object); K7 Operating from moving aircraft/vehicle; K8 Alcohol or drugs; K9 Daylight operations; K10 VLOS; K11 Visual observer; K12 Prohibition of multiple sUAS; K13 Hazardous material; K14 Right-of-way (K14a see and avoid); K15 Operations over human beings; K16 Prior authorization in certain airspace; K17 Vicinity of airports; K18 Prohibited or restricted areas; K19 NOTAM flight restrictions; K20 Preflight familiarization, inspection, actions; K21 Operating limitations (K21a max groundspeed; K21b altitude; K21c minimum visibility; K21d cloud clearance); K22 Requirements for the certificate; K23 Automated operations; K24 Civil twilight operations; K25 Night operations; K26 Transportation of property; K27 ATC transponder prohibition; K28 ADS-B Out prohibition.
- **I.C Remote Pilot Certification** (subpart C): K1 Alcohol/drug offenses; K2 Refusing a drug or alcohol test; K3 Eligibility; K4 Aeronautical knowledge recency.
- **I.D Waivers** (subpart D, sic — now subpart E): K1 Waiver policy and requirements.
- **I.E Operations Over People**: K1 Remote pilot responsibilities; K2 Operations over people at night; K3 Categories (K3a–d Category 1–4); K4 Selecting an operational area; K5 Minimum distances from a person; K6 Over moving vehicles; K7 Modifications; K8 Closed and restricted access sites; K9 Remote pilot operating instructions; K10 Required components and Category declaration; K11 Optional components; K12 Applicant produced/designed/modified sUAS; K13 Declaration of Compliance; K14 Maintenance of an OOP-eligible sUAS; K15 Means of Compliance; K16 Impact kinetic energy; K17 Exposed rotating parts.
- **I.F Remote Identification** (part 89): K1 Standard RID; K2 Alternative RID; K3 Aeronautical research operations; K4 ADS-B Out; K5 Confirmation of identification; K6 Minimum message elements; K7 Product labeling.

**II. Airspace Classification and Operating Requirements** (ACS 15–25%; CIB 20%)
- **II.A Airspace Classification**: K1 General airspace (K1a Class B; K1b C; K1c D; K1d E; K1e G uncontrolled); K2 Special use airspace (prohibited, restricted, warning, MOA, alert, CFA); K3 Other airspace areas (airport advisory services, MTRs, TFRs, parachute jump, TRSAs, NSAs, VFR routes); K4 ATC and the NAS.
- **II.B Airspace Operational Requirements** (refs include SAFO 10015): K1 Basic weather minimums; K2 ATC authorizations; K3 Operations near airports; K4 Potential flight hazards (K4a accident causal factors; K4b avoid flight beneath unmanned balloons; K4c emergency airborne inspection; K4d precipitation static; K4e lasers and reporting illumination; K4f thermal plumes; K4g wire environment); K5 NOTAM system incl. obtaining NOTAMs via Flight Service; K6 Operator equipment for night flight; K7 Ground structures and structure lighting; K8 Unlit ground hazards; K9 Manned aircraft lighting; K10 sUAS lighting requirements.

**III. Weather** (ACS 11–16%; CIB 5%)
- **III.A Sources of Weather**: K1 Internet briefing and sources; K2 METAR; K3 TAF; K4 Weather charts; K5 ASOS and AWOS.
- **III.B Effects of Weather on Performance**: K1 (a density altitude; b wind and currents; c stability, pressure, temperature; d air masses and fronts; e thunderstorms and microbursts; f tornadoes; g icing; h hail; i fog; j ceiling and visibility; k lightning).

**IV. Loading and Performance** (ACS 7–11%; CIB 2%)
- **IV.A**: K1 General loading and performance (K1a effects of loading changes; K1b balance, stability, CG); K2 Use of performance data.

**V. Operations** (ACS 35–45%; CIB 25%)
- **V.A Radio Communications**: K1 Towered/non-towered airport operations; K2 CTAF; K3 Self-announce procedures; K4 UNICOM; K5 ATIS; K6 Call signs and registration numbers; K7 Phonetic alphabet; K8 Phraseology: altitudes, directions, speed, time.
- **V.B Airport Operations** (refs AC 150/5200-32): K1 Types of airports incl. heliports, seaplane bases; K2 Monitoring ATC; K3 Runway markings and signage; K4 Traffic patterns; K5 SIDA; K6 Airport data sources (K6a charts; K6b Chart Supplements); K7 Wildlife hazards and strike reporting; K8 Airport and seaplane base lighting.
- **V.C Emergency Procedures** (SAFOs 09013, 10017, 15010): K1 Emergency planning and communication; K2 Lithium batteries (K2a transport; K2b charging; K2c usage; K2d fire risks); K3 Lost link and fly-aways; K4 GPS loss; K5 Frequency spectrums; K6 OOP procedures; K7 Night procedures.
- **V.D ADM** (FAA-H-8083-2, -25): K1 ADM (K1a team communication; K1b task management); K2 CRM; K3 Situational awareness; K4 Hazardous attitudes; K5 Hazard identification and risk assessment.
- **V.E Physiology**: K1 dehydration, heatstroke etc.; K2 Drugs and alcohol; K3 Prescription/OTC medication; K4 Hyperventilation; K5 Stress and fatigue; K6 Factors affecting vision; K7 Fitness for flight; K8 Night physiology; K9 Night illusions.
- **V.F Maintenance and Inspection**: K1 Basic maintenance; K2 Preflight inspection; K3 Mitigating mechanical failures (battery, control devices); K4 Record keeping; K5 Who may perform maintenance; K6 Night preflight; K7 Manufacturer's DoC for Category 2 and 3.

The regulation's own list of knowledge areas (§107.73(a)–(m), 13 areas) and the part-61
training list (§107.74(a)–(h), 8 areas) are a secondary checklist.

## 7. Confusions and traps

1. **Old study material predates 2021.** SG (2016) lists 12 knowledge areas (no night
   operations), references AC 107-2 (cancelled) and ACS-10. Pre-2021 materials say night
   flight needs a waiver and flight over people is prohibited without waiver; recurrent
   *test* (UGR) no longer exists (FAA 5 Apr 2021). Night waivers issued before 21 Apr 2021
   terminated 17 May 2021 (§107.29(d)).
2. **§107.5 is gone.** Falsification is now 14 CFR §3.403 (effective 3 Nov 2025, FR
   2025-16902). ACS I.A.K3 still tests the concept. §107.160(d)(2)(ii) still cross-refers
   to "§ 107.5(a)" — a stale reference in the CFR itself.
3. **"Accident reporting" vs "Safety event reporting".** §107.9 retitled 9 Dec 2022; AC
   107-2A says "within 10 days" and "$500 to repair or replace (whichever is lower)". The
   reg: exempt if repair cost ≤ $500 **or** fair market value ≤ $500.
4. **ACS subpart label.** ACS Task I.D says waivers are "subpart D"; in the current CFR
   subpart D is Operations Over Human Beings and waivers are subpart E.
5. **Two weightings** (ACS ranges vs CIB 2025 JTA). Do not state one as "the" weighting
   without date and source.
6. **Area forecast (FA)** appears in the SG and PHAK 25C; AIM 7-1-4 says the GFA is
   "designed to replace the legacy FA".
7. **Sectional revision cycle:** SG/PHAK "revised semiannually" vs FAA product page
   "updated every 56 days".
8. **NOTAM expansion:** current AIM (Change 3, 7/9/2026) says "Notice to Airmen (NOTAM)
   System". Some 2022–2025 material says "Notice to Air Missions" (not verified here — §10).
9. **Registration thresholds:** 0.55 lb exemption applies only to §44809 recreational
   flyers; part 107 aircraft must register at any weight; Category 1 is also 0.55 lb —
   easy to conflate.
10. **Age 14 vs 16:** test at 14; certificate at 16.
11. **Recurrent is training, not a test**, and is free online (ALC-677; ALC-515 for part 61).
12. **Controlled airspace:** Class E needs authorization only in the **surface area**
    designated for an airport (§107.41); ATCA sample 2026 turns on exactly this.
13. **Waivers vs rules:** VLOS and moving-vehicle waivers never for carrying others'
    property for hire; right-of-way waiver covers only §107.37(a), not (b).
14. **In-flight emergency report** only "upon request"; the 10-day report is separate.
15. **Category 3 vs open-air assemblies:** Cat 3 never; Cat 1/2/4 allowed only with RID
    compliance (§89.110/§89.115(a)).
16. **RID message differences:** standard RID broadcasts **control station** location;
    broadcast module broadcasts **take-off location**; module/FRIA require keeping the UA
    in sight.
17. **Anti-collision lighting:** may dim, never extinguish; not required in daylight.
18. **VLOS:** FPV doesn't count; binoculars only momentarily; VO optional.
19. **IMSAFE "E":** SG = Emotion; AC 107-2A App. A = Eating. Cite which.
20. **Weather units:** METAR wind is true; runway numbers magnetic; cloud heights AGL;
    visibility SM; TAF radius 5 SM.
21. **Transponder/ADS-B:** part 107 ops must not transmit them unless authorized; ADS-B
    cannot satisfy RID.
22. **Testing supplements** are being replaced by embedded images; UAG image questions from
    current charts from 26 Oct 2026 (date corrected from 27 Oct).
23. **Proposed BVLOS rule (part 108)** is only an NPRM (FR 7 Aug 2025; comment reopenings
    Jan–Feb 2026); no final rule found in the Federal Register search. Don't teach it as law.

## 8. Naming

- **FAA branding (Order 1700.6D, effective 02/27/2024):** "Only FAA organizations may use
  the FAA logo, unless approved by the Assistant Administrator for Communications." and
  the logo "may not be used in any way that implies endorsement by the FAA, DOT, or the
  United States government of any product, service, or enterprise ... or which appears to
  sanction the activities of a third party."
  (https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Order/Order_1700.6D.pdf)
- No trademark claim on "Remote Pilot Certificate", "Part 107" or "UAG" was found; these
  are regulatory names.
- **Applying CONTENT-POLICY §4:** plain-text factual naming only, brand leads, no FAA logo
  or DOT seal, no "official/FAA-approved". Suggested title: "[Site name] deck for the FAA
  Remote Pilot (Part 107) knowledge test (UAG)". The "Everyone else" notice wording
  ("is a trademark of") does not fit a government certificate; suggested footer: "This
  deck is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed or approved by the Federal
  Aviation Administration or the U.S. Department of Transportation." FAA is not in §5's
  restricted list. `ExamRefs` to ACS codes seem permissible (the ACS is public domain) —
  owner to confirm under §2.3/§3.
- Policy §3 already places "the FAA question bank (confidential)" in tier C: build from
  handbooks. Treat the ATCA/PSI sample questions as out of scope for card content.

## 9. Languages

- **Exam language: English.** The applicant "must demonstrate the ability to read, write,
  speak, and understand the English language" "throughout the application and testing
  process" (ACS App. 1, Aviation English Language Standard, p.A-2); §107.61(b) makes it an
  eligibility condition. No FAA or PSI page fetched mentions the UAG in any other language.
- **Translations:** the FAA's Spanish UAS page (https://www.faa.gov/uas/espanol/para_empezar,
  updated 21 July 2025) links to the remote-pilot section marked "(en inglés)": "Pilotos a
  distancia certificados, incluyendo los operadores comerciales (en inglés)". No official
  or openly licensed translation of Part 107, AC 107-2A, the ACS or the Study Guide was
  found. Any FAA Spanish pages are US Gov works (tier B), but only general safety content.
