# psm-ii audit, part b (topics 4, 5, 6)

Cards checked: 138 (T4 41, T5 63, T6 34). Scenario keys and choicesExplained
letters all match their choices. No card states PSM II exam facts, the
"stances" model or cites www.scrum.org. The three writer-made primers
(empirical product planning, learning cycle, Scrum adoption) stay within the
Guide's use of the words; the first two get a note that the Guide does not
define them. Repeats were checked against the fronts and backs of the
`scrum-guide` and `scrum-pspo` decks. Every deleted card's concept keeps an
application card. Source: Scrum Guide 2020 (scrumguides.org), cached copy.
