## Checks with no finding
- **Letters:** all 21 scenario cards have choices A–D. Each key's letter and text match its option, and every choicesExplained letter names the option it describes. No capital letter inside a word was changed, and every lone A–D is either a label or the article "A"/"An".
- **Certifying bodies:** no card names ASQ, CSSBB or any certifying body. "Certified 500 g mass" in lssbb.measure-data.bias is a calibration term.
- **OpenStax (tier D):** nothing looks drawn from it. The formulas, notation and examples follow NIST or Wikipedia wording, or are original.
- **Formulas, constants and bands:** all match their sources. These include the mean, the median rule for an even count, s² with N − 1, s, the range, the IQR and the 1.5/3.0 IQR fences, and z = (x − μ)/σ. They also include σ/√n, 68/95/99.7, the addition, multiplication and conditional rules, the CLT wording, t ≈ normal for ν > 30, the chi-square and F construction, and the Poisson mean = variance. Binomial npq = np(1 − p) agrees with the NIST SD √(np(1 − p)). The %GRR bands are 1%/9% (% contribution) and 10%/30% (study variation and tolerance), and the resolution rule is one-tenth.
- **"Citation needed" and "disputed" tags:** none sits on a passage these cards rely on. The tags in the Level of measurement, Sampling bias, IQR, CLT, Standard error, Binomial and Skewness articles are all away from the cited passages. The only affected card is Bessel's correction, above.
- **Scenario judgements:** each one follows from its source (NVA-but-necessary, spaghetti map, discrete, ordinal, stratified, convenience, subgroup size 1, the 6% band, equipment for repeatability, attribute agreement, symmetric unimodal, the 47-minute outlier, CLT means, 95%, z = +3, 0.13, binomial, Poisson, chi-square, F, departure from normality). None is disputable.
- **Terms taught later:** outside the three GRR cards, topic 4 uses no variance or standard-deviation term. "Average" appears only as an everyday word.
