# faa-cfi audit C: topic 9, Teaching manoeuvres (09-teaching-manoeuvres.json)

411 cards checked (281 basic, 66 cloze, 64 scenario). All 64 scenario cards: the choicesExplained letters match the choices, every distractor is explained and none explains the key. Twelve backs differ from their keyed choice only by a closing full stop; the checker checks keys, so they are not reported. I compared every common-error card with the numbered lists in the Airplane Flying Handbook, FAA-H-8083-3C: ch. 3 pp. 3-9, 3-17, 3-20 and 3-24; ch. 5 pp. 5-7, 5-12, 5-21, 5-26 and 5-27; ch. 6 pp. 6-6 to 6-14; ch. 7 pp. 7-7 to 7-18; ch. 9 pp. 9-10 to 9-27; ch. 10 pp. 10-3 to 10-7. I also checked the "all four lists" and "only this list" claims. The error cards are sound. I read the cross-control, elevator trim, secondary stall and spin-procedure passages (AFH pp. 5-18 to 5-26) and the relevant parts of ACS-25: X.B, F to I, XIII.C and Appendices 2 and 3. I also read 14 CFR 61.183(i) and 61.49(b). Both primers teach terms that are new at this level (the region of reversed command is a topic 9 term in faa-cfi-terms.json and appears in no prerequisite deck), so neither re-teaches a term. No deleted card needs a `conceptIDs` patch, because each deleted card's concept is already on the card that is kept.
