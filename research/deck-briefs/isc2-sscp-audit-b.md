# isc2-sscp audit B: topics 10–17

Scope: decks/isc2-sscp/notes/10-assessment-and-vulnerability-management.json to 17-networking-fundamentals.json, 407 cards. I checked each card against its `evidence` first. Claims that go beyond the evidence were checked in research/sources/isc2-sscp/ (SP 800-34r1, 800-40r4, 800-52r2, 800-57pt1r5, 800-61r3, 800-63B-4, 800-77r1, 800-86, 800-92, 800-94, 800-115, 800-122, 800-177r1, 800-215, FIPS 180-4, 186-5, 197-upd1, 198-1, 203, the IANA port registry). I also compared the non-scenario cards with the isc2-cc deck (decks/isc2-cc/notes) to find cards that re-teach what CC already teaches.

Numbers checked and correct: AES block and key sizes (FIPS 197); SHA digest range of 160 to 512 bits and the 2^64-bit input limit for SHA-256 (FIPS 180-4); 3072-bit RSA and ECC f = 256–383 for 128 bits of strength, and 1024-bit RSA at 80 bits or less (SP 800-57 Table 2); security strengths {80, 112, 128, 192, 256}, with 80 no longer enough; salt of at least 32 bits (SP 800-63B-4); syslog severities 0–7; the volatile-data order (SP 800-86); TLS 1.3 by 1 January 2024 and no SSL 2.0/3.0 (SP 800-52r2); IKE on UDP 4500; and every port card against the IANA registry (20, 21, 22, 23, 25, 53, 69, 80, 88, 110, 115, 123, 143, 161/162, 389, 443, 514/udp, 636, 993, 995). Every scenario's choicesExplained letters match its choices (`build/answer-letters.mjs` reports 0 cards; I checked each by hand too).

Totals: 34 findings: wrong 0, unsupported 2, ambiguous 0, unclear 6, minor 26. The fixes file has 34 lines, patching 27 findings (16 of them deletions of cards that re-teach isc2-cc). Two need research. Five minor re-teach findings on primers are logged without a patch. `node build/apply-fixes.mjs isc2-sscp research/deck-briefs/isc2-sscp-fixes-b.jsonl --dry` reports no broken cards.

**Re-teaching isc2-cc.** 16 cards ask the same question as an isc2-cc card and give the same answer, taken from the same NIST sentence. I delete them. Each concept they carried still has other cards in this deck. The only exception is `sscp.sym-vs-asym-use`, which moves to scenario-rsa-size. Where a card repeats CC but adds something CC lacks, I kept it or refocused it (event-filtering). Five primers (log aggregation, log normalisation, full backup, incremental backup, UDP) define a term that an isc2-cc fact card already defines. The terms registry assigns each of these terms to an SSCP topic and later cards use it, so deleting the primer would leave those terms undefined. I have logged them for the deck owner and not patched them.
