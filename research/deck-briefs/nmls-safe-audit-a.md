## Findings

safe.tila-hoepa-lo.rescission-deadline-scenario | wrong | choicesExplained gives the wrong reasons. Monday (A) comes from counting Sunday, not from "skipping Saturday". Three calendar days would also give Monday, not the following Friday (D). | § 1026.2(a)(6): "all calendar days except Sundays and the legal public holidays"
safe.trid-disclosures.le-seven-day-scenario | wrong | choicesExplained says that by Monday "only three business days will have passed". Counting from a Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday and Monday make four. | comment 19(e)(1)(iii)-2: "Saturday is a business day, pursuant to § 1026.2(a)(6)"
safe.tila-hoepa-lo.what-is-dual-compensation | unsupported | The back ("no one else may pay a loan originator") and the explanation ("rules out every other source of pay") overstate the ban. They also contradict the later card dual-comp-org-pays-lo. | § 1026.36(d)(2)(i)(C): "the loan originator organization may pay compensation to an individual loan originator ... subject to paragraph (d)(1)"
safe.trid-disclosures.trigger-construction | unsupported | The back says "the original said so clearly", which reads as if the Loan Estimate had to predict the late settlement. The rule requires a different statement: that revised disclosures may be issued up to 60 days before consummation. | § 1026.19(e)(3)(iv)(F): "state clearly and conspicuously that at any time prior to 60 days before consummation, the creditor may issue revised disclosures"
safe.tila-hoepa-lo.hpml-escrow-cancel-scenario | unclear | choicesExplained says "A and B each meet only one condition". In fact the borrower meets both of those conditions; what she fails is the 80 percent test. | § 1026.35(b)(3)(ii)(A): "The unpaid principal balance is less than 80 percent of the original value"
safe.respa-ecoa.transfer-15-days | minor | The back gives the 15-day timing as absolute. In listed cases either notice may come up to 30 days after the effective date. | § 1024.33(b)(3)(ii): "not more than 30 days after the effective date of the transfer ... in any case in which the transfer of servicing is preceded by" termination for cause, bankruptcy, or FDIC/NCUA proceedings
safe.tila-hoepa-lo.hpml-appraisal-interior | minor | The card presents the interior-appraisal rule as applying to every HPML, but qualified mortgages and some other loans are exempt. | § 1026.35(c)(3)(i): "Except as provided in paragraph (c)(2)"; (c)(2)(i): "A loan that satisfies the criteria of a qualified mortgage"
safe.trid-disclosures.what-is-your-home-loan-toolkit | minor | The back names the publisher "Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection". The toolkit uses only "Consumer Financial Protection Bureau", the name newcomers know. | toolkit p. 2 and back cover: "Consumer Financial Protection Bureau"; "A step-by-step guide"
