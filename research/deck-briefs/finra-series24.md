## Traps

- `[T1]` Retake waiting periods changed in July 2026. They were 30 days, and
  180 days after three failures in two years (the rule as described in FR
  2026-14262 #page=1). Now they are 15 and 60 days. Older materials give 30
  and 180.
- `[T2]` **Rule 3290 replaces Rules 3270 and 3280.** The SEC approved it on
  18 September 2026: "The proposed rule change would replace current FINRA
  Rules 3270 and 3280 with new FINRA Rule 3290". FINRA "would determine an
  effective date" (FR 2026-19126). Until that date, 3270/3280 remain the rules
  and the exam may test either. Cards on these concepts must be volatile.
- `[T2]` **Gift limit (Rule 3220).** The SEC approved an increase "from $100
  to $300 per person per year" (FR 2026-03127, 18 February 2026). The
  effective date was not checked.
- `[T2]` 10b5-1 cooling-off periods: for directors and officers, the later of
  90 days or two business days after the results, capped at 120 days. For
  other persons (not the issuer), 30 days (Rule 10b5-1, 2025 CFR).
- `[T3]` Securities Act "Section 4(6)" is now 4(a)(5). Pub. L. 111–203
  "redesignated par. (6) as (5)", and Pub. L. 112–106 designated the
  existing provisions "as subsec. (a)" (15 U.S.C. 77d notes).
- `[T4]` Rule 15c3-2 is "[Reserved]" in the 2025 CFR. Older lists still cite
  it for free credit balances.
- `[T4]` Early warning (Rule 17a-11): net capital below 120 percent of the
  minimum, notice within 24 hours. Do not confuse this with the minimum
  itself ($250,000 for a firm that carries customer accounts and holds their
  funds or securities, Rule 15c3-1(a)(2)(i)).
- `[T5]` Regulation S-P now requires an "incident response program"
  (§ 248.30, history note "[89 FR 47786, June 3, 2024]"). Compliance dates
  were not checked.
- `[T6]` Settlement is T+1: "later than the first business day after the date
  of the contract" (Rule 15c6-1, history note "[88 FR 13952, Mar. 6, 2023]").
- `[T6]` Regulation NMS: Rule 612 now sets the minimum pricing increment by a
  stock's "Time Weighted Average Quoted Spread" (2025 CFR). The 605–612 pages
  carry 2024 amendment notes. Check the compliance dates.
- `[T7]` Rule 504 limit: "shall not exceed $10,000,000" (2025 CFR). Series 7
  may already teach this. Check for figures from older materials.
- `[T7]` Schedule 13D: "within five business days after the date of the
  acquisition" (Rule 13d-1(a), 2025 CFR).
- `[T7]` The Rule 105 restricted period is "the shorter of" five business days
  before pricing, or the time from the initial filing to pricing.
- `[T8]` Rules 137, 138 and 139 are easily swapped. They cover,
  respectively, a broker "not participating" in the distribution, research
  on other securities, and a broker distributing the securities (CFR
  headings).

- **How the FR sources were chosen.** Each was found through the Federal
  Register API by rule number and title. The pick was the most recent SEC
  notice or order on a FINRA filing whose title names the rule, or
  (where noted in `build.py`) a page on which a phrase for the concept
  appears. Every cited page was machine-checked to contain the rule number
  or phrase, but was not read in full. Some picks are narrow amendments
  (for example, the Rule 3110 pick is a 2024 notice about Form U4). Writers must check
  that the cited page supports each card and report any that do not.
- **ecfr.gov blocks automated clients** (robots.txt disallows
  `/api/versioner/v1/full/`; `/current/` redirects to
  unblock.federalregister.gov). The CFR text is therefore the annual
  edition revised to 1 April 2025. Later amendments are not reflected.

  statement was found.

- Nasdaq rules come only from SEC notices of Nasdaq filings (one pick, 4613).
