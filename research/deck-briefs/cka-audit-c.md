Scope: decks/cka/notes/09-* to 12-* (95 + 66 + 44 + 72 cards). Cards checked against their evidence and the cached text in research/sources/cka/.
Version check: every kubernetes.io page cited in topics 9–12 was also fetched from the archived v1.35 docs (v1-35.docs.kubernetes.io), and each card's evidence was compared with it.
