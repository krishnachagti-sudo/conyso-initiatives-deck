# istqb-ctfl audit, part c: topics 5–6 (280 cards)

Scope: decks/istqb-ctfl/notes/05-black-box-techniques.json (150 cards) and 06-white-box-experience-collaboration.json (130 cards). The 19 glossary cards (tier B) were checked against their evidence and research/sources/istqb-ctfl/; the black-box and white-box synonyms on the primer backs are in the glossary records' "synonyms" field. The 261 syllabus cards (tier C) were checked against the saved v4.0.1 syllabus text (sections 2.1.3, 4.1–4.5 and Appendix C, pp. 26, 39–46, 73, 75). Page numbers are all right.
Scenarios worked out: every EP partition count, Each Choice minimum, 2- and 3-value BVA set, BVA/EP/decision-table/branch percentage, full-table column count (2³ = 8, 2¹⁰ = 1,024), the document-workflow state model (all states 1 test, valid transitions 1 test, 3/4 = 75%, 4 × 4 = 16 cells with 12 invalid, the masking test) and the statement and branch cases for the member-discount code. All answers are correct. The parcel front is ambiguous (see below), and one choice explanation gives a wrong reason.
Closeness: fronts and backs that copy a whole syllabus sentence, or nearly all of one, are logged as minor and reworded. Short fixed terms, the user-story template and list items were left alone. One scenario (3-value BVA) repeats the syllabus's own worked example with the sign flipped, so it has a new defect.
