# cka audit A: topics 01–04

Scope: decks/cka/notes/01-kubectl-techniques.json to 04-rbac-and-access.json, 294 cards. I checked every card against its `evidence` first. Claims that go beyond the evidence were checked in research/sources/cka/ (imperative commands, kubectl create, quick reference, install-kubeadm, create-cluster-kubeadm, kubeadm init/join, container runtimes, ports, kubeadm upgrade, kubeadm certs, safely drain, configure-upgrade-etcd, HA topology, high availability, RBAC, authorization, CSRs, client CSR task, service accounts, kubeconfig).

The cache has no text for the two Linux Foundation pages cited by the 11 exam-environment cards (the manifest path is null). I fetched them once, with a generic User-Agent and no personal details: tips-cka-and-ckad and certification-resources-allowed. They support every exam card: "Nested ssh is not supported", "sudo -i", the base system "does not have any of the above tools pre-installed", "kubectl with k alias and Bash autocompletion", "currently running Kubernetes v1.35", and "using the search function on https://kubernetes.io/docs/ is allowed, but you must not open external search results". Tier C (CONTENT-POLICY.md §3), so the cards use facts in our own words.

**v1.35 check.** No card teaches anything newer than v1.35. Every version-sensitive feature a card uses predates v1.35: kubeadm:cluster-admins (v1.29), v1beta4 caCertificateValidityPeriod (v1.31), expirationSeconds (v1.22) and the swap settings. The cached container-runtimes page says that "In Kubernetes 1.37, with the KubeletCgroupDriverFromCRI feature gate enabled", the kubelet ignores `cgroupDriver`. No card teaches that behaviour, and the cgroup cards stay correct without it. The etcd restore card is dated for v1.35 but repeats the docs' "slated for removal from etcd v3.6". This needs research (see below).
