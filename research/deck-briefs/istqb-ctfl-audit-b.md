# istqb-ctfl audit B: topics 03–04

Scope: decks/istqb-ctfl/notes/03-lifecycle.json (197 cards) and 04-static-testing.json (135 cards), 332 cards. Glossary cards (tier B) were checked against their `evidence` and research/sources/istqb-ctfl/; every glossary back matches its definition. Syllabus cards (tier C, CONTENT-POLICY.md §3) were checked against the saved CTFL v4.0.1 syllabus, pp. 25–37 and Appendix C p. 75. Every syllabus fact checks out apart from the items below. Most findings are wording: many backs copy syllabus clauses almost word for word, which tier C forbids. Card-by-card lists of named items (factor names, attribute names, level and type names) were left alone, because they are exam terms rather than prose. Pool cards: only the explanation and choicesExplained were checked.
