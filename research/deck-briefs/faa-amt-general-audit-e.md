# faa-amt-general audit, part e (topic 10: regulations, maintenance forms, records and publications)

Cards checked: 308 (273 basic, 31 scenario, 4 cloze). Each card was checked against its evidence. Claims that went beyond the evidence were checked in the saved sources: the current eCFR text of parts 39, 43 and 91, the 14 CFR 1.1 definitions, FAA-H-8083-30B, FAA-S-ACS-1 and AC 43.13-1B Change 1. Every section number and period checked matches the rule text: 43.3(d), (g) and (k); 43.5; 43.7(b) and (f); the contents of 43.9(a) and (d) and of 43.11(a) and (b); 43.13; 43.15; 43.16; Appendix A's major alterations (i)–(xiii) and (i)–(vi) and major repairs (i)–(xxviii), (i)–(iii), (i)–(xiv) and (i)–(v); preventive maintenance items (1)–(31) (the current list has no (32)); Appendix B (a duplicate copy, 48 hours, triplicate for fuel tanks); 91.403, 91.405, 91.407, 91.409 (12 calendar months, 100 hours, the 10-hour overrun, progressive inspections), 91.411 and 91.413 (24 calendar months), 91.417 (1 year, transfer on sale, the Form 337 kept on board) and 91.421; and 39.5, 39.13, 39.15–39.27.
