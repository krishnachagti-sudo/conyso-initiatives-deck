# AWS Cloud Practitioner: notes for every writer (read after DIGEST.md)

- **Slug:** `aws-ccp`. **Family prefix:** `awsccp` (card ids `awsccp.<topic-slug>.<card-slug>`, concept ids `awsccp.<concept-slug>`). Use the concept ids in `aws-ccp-concepts.json`; add new ones only when a card needs them.
- **Builds on:** nothing. Every term gets its primer.
- **Sources, their tiers and licence labels.** The label must match src/licences.json for the host. Tier A or B: "B · <licence>", with evidence. Tier C: "C · facts only, in our own words", with no evidence.
  - docs.aws.amazon.com → tier B ("The materials hosted on docs.aws.amazon.com are licensed as follows: documentat)
- **Evidence is required.** The texts are in `research/sources/aws-ccp/`; the slice prints a passage under each concept.
- **Volatile facts:** `volatile: true`, `validAsOf: "2026-09-26 · AWS documentation as published"`.
- **Scenarios:** your own. Never reuse a source's worked examples or exercises.
- **Deck notice:** already in deck.json.
