# KCNA deck audit, part A (topics 01–08)

Auditor brief: research/deck-briefs/AUDIT.md. Scope: decks/kcna/notes/01…08, read in `order` (706 cards: 74 + 109 + 87 + 169 + 76 + 70 + 55 + 66). I checked every card against the saved sources in the scratch folder (CNCF glossary repo, K8s docs markdown, NIST CSRC pages, RFCs, CNCF TOC definition, OpenGitOps glossary, K8s releases page). I fetched only to check whether cited URLs are live. I also viewed all six figures used in these topics (container-evolution.png, components-of-kubernetes.png, pod-phases.svg, probe-timeline.svg, deployment-replicaset-pods.svg, taint-effects.svg).

Counts (one line per problem card; a card can appear on more than one line): 107 lines across 101 cards. By severity: wrong 0, unsupported 20, ambiguous 2, unclear 32, minor 53.

## Checks the assignment asked for

- **Wikipedia sources:** no card in topics 01–08 cites Wikipedia in `source` or `sourceURL`. Computing basics are cited to NIST CSRC, IETF RFCs and yaml.org, which are primary sources. Nothing to flag.
- **T4 "what Kubernetes provides" cards (known issue): confirmed.** Seven cards come before the terms they use are taught: provides-rollouts, -bin-packing, -dual-stack, -secret-config, -batch, -storage and -self-healing. The table below gives the card that teaches each term and where to move each card. Three cards can stay where they are, because their terms are already taught: provides-discovery, provides-horizontal and provides-extensibility.
- **Dead source links (not asked, but the most serious finding):** seven CNCF glossary entries are marked `draft: true` / `status: Deprecated` in the glossary repo, so their pages return 404. They are PaaS, SaaS, DBaaS, Managed Services, Firewall, Version Control and CaaS. Together they are cited by 17 cards. The NIST DNS URL also returns 404; the correct page is …/domain_name_system_dns.
- **Facts checked with no error found:** all numbers and thresholds, including pull-policy defaults, backoff 10 s–300 s and the 10-minute reset, probe defaults, HTTP 200–399, maxSurge/maxUnavailable rounding, revisionHistoryLimit 10, backoffLimit 6, the CronJob 52/63-character limits, 1 MiB for ConfigMaps and Secrets, 253/63-character name limits, version skew, support window, release dates, PriorityClass 1 billion, and eviction thresholds.
