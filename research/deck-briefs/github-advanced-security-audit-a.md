## Findings

ghas.licensing-billing.what-is-an-active-committer | ambiguous | Back says "a licensed member", but external collaborators and pending invitees also count | "they are also a member, an enterprise-managed user, an external collaborator, or have a pending invitation to join your organization or enterprise" (billing page, Active and unique committers)
ghas.secret-scanning-alerts.scn-partner-leak | ambiguous | On a public repository secret scanning runs anyway, so a user alert could show in the tab; the front has to say that user alerts are off for D to be the only answer. The explanation for B is patched to match | "Public repositories: Secret scanning runs automatically for free." (Secret scanning, How can I access this feature); "Secret scanning alerts for users can be enabled on any free public repository that you own." (Enable secret scanning); "You cannot change the configuration of secret scanning for partner patterns on public repositories." (Secret scanning for partners)
ghas.secret-scanning-alerts.scn-who-notified | ambiguous | Keyed answer "an organisation owner who does not administer that repository" cannot exist: organisation owners have admin access to every repository in the organisation (github-foundations teaches this). Changed to enterprise owner | "Organization owners and enterprise owners, if they are administrators of repositories where secrets were leaked" (Monitoring alerts, Incremental scans); "Organization owners have complete administrative access to your organization." (Roles in an organization)
ghas.push-protection.sc-limit-existing | ambiguous | "Existing secrets" means secrets that already have alerts. Alerts in an old, imported repository do not make secrets existing in the new protected repository. The explanation for A ("not stated in the Docs") also gives no reason | "If a push contains over 1,000 existing secrets (that is, secrets for which alerts have already been created), push protection will not block the push." (Secret scanning scope, Push protection limitations)
ghas.push-protection.limit-five-shown | unsupported | Explanation "You see the rest only after dealing with those" is not in the source | "we will only show you the first five (we will always show you a maximum of five secrets at one time)" (Secret scanning scope, Push protection limitations)
