# Audit B: faa-part-107, topics 07–11

Auditor part: `decks/faa-part-107/notes/07…11`, 465 cards (07: 64, 08: 90, 09: 99, 10: 108, 11: 104), read in `order`.
For pass 2 I assumed the reader had studied topics 1–6 (their `introduces` lists plus the deck glossary).
I checked every claim against the saved sources in the scratch folder: part107.txt, ac107-2a.txt, study_guide.txt, aim_7_1 / aim_5_1 / aim_chap4_section_3 / aim_chap7_section_6, PHAK ch. 1, 2, 12–16, cug.txt and cugp/, authz.txt and sectional.txt.

Nothing was found **wrong**. Every number, threshold, code decode and chart reading matches its source.
