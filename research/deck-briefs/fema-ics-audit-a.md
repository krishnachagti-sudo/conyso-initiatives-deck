# fema-ics audit A: topics 01 to 10 (IS-100.c and IS-200.c)

Scope: 601 cards in `decks/fema-ics/notes/01-*.json` to `10-*.json`. `image` fields were skipped because figures are being added in parallel.
Sources: the saved copies in the scratch folder (`SM100c`, `SM200c`, `IG200c`, `SMIS0700b`, `NIMS2017`, `NIMS2017_ISLM`). Page numbers are PDF pages unless a line says SM- or IG-.
Special checks:
- **2017 terminology.** One card conflicts with the NIMS 2017 glossary (assisting agency). One NIMS 2017 title row is missing (Single Resource Boss). Three abbreviations (SOFR, LNO, OSC) are not in NIMS 2017 or either manual. No 2008-era terms were found: there is no "ICS Management Characteristics" used as a current name, no five-component NIMS, no MACS and no NRP.
- **Scenario originality.** Two cards copy course activities: the Rosa off-duty EMT scenario (IS-100.c Activity 2.2) and the windstorm scenario (IS-200.c Activity 3.2). A third, classify-type4, copies Activity 6.2 Scenario 4 and also gets the answer wrong. The course's worked examples are reused in several more cards.
- **Set completeness.** Every "(N of M)" group has exactly M cards. Four sets leave out members of the source list: support titles, the Operational Period Briefing agenda, the size-up list and the initial briefing list. The Delegation of Authority set merges 10 source items into 8.

Format: `id | pass | severity | problem | source text or plainer wording`
