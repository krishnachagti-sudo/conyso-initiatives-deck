## Naming

- **FINRA's marks.** FINRA's trademark list includes "Securities Industry
  Essentials®" and "SIE®" (https://www.finra.org/trademarks). It says: "Third
  parties may not use the FINRA Marks, in any manner, which suggests or hints
  at affiliation with, sponsorship of or endorsement by, FINRA without prior
  written permission." The site footer: "FINRA is a Registered Trademark of the
  Financial Industry Regulatory Authority, Inc."
- **Terms of Use** (https://www.finra.org/terms-of-use): site works "may not be
  copied, reproduced… without FINRA's prior written consent, except to the
  extent that such use constitutes 'fair use'"; content "shall be used ONLY for
  your own non-commercial personal or professional use"; no "data mining,
  scraping or harvesting tools (including robots)" or bulk copying. Links are
  allowed if nothing "create[s] the appearance that an entity is associated
  with or sponsored by FINRA". FINRA is not in CONTENT-POLICY §5, so the deck
  may name the exam.
- **§4 applies as the "Everyone else" row.** Plain text, our brand first, a
  relational phrase, no logo: `[Site name] deck for the Securities Industry
  Essentials (SIE) exam`. Never "SIE Flashcards", never "official". A URL path
  such as `/decks/finra/sie/` is acceptable under §4; never in a domain.
- **§2.3:** the outline is all rights reserved ("© 2025 FINRA"). Link to it and
  describe coverage in our own topic labels; never reproduce its bullets.
- **Deck notice text:**

## Traps

- **Settlement is T+1.** Rule 15c6-1 now requires settlement no "later than the first business day after the date of the contract". The investor.gov glossary page "Settling Securities Transactions, T+2" still says "two business days". Cite eCFR, never that page. `[T6]`
- **Gift limit is $300 under FINRA Rule 3220,** "Amended by SR-FINRA-2025-003 eff. Mar. 30, 2026". Study material written before that date shows an older figure. `[T9]`
- **MSRB G-20 has two versions until 1 December 2026:** the current rule says $300, and the legacy rule ($100) still applies to "all municipal advisors as well as dealers that are not FINRA members (i.e., bank dealers), until December 1, 2026". `[T9]`
- **75 vs 80.** 75 scored items, 80 on screen (5 unscored pretest). `[T1]` exam-facts card only.
- **Four years vs two years.** SIE results last four years (FAQ; Rule 1210.08 "expiration of SIE"); a lapsed representative registration needs a new qualification exam after two years (Rule 1210.08). `[T9]`
- **CE Regulatory Element is annual** (by 31 December each year) under Rule 1240 as amended from 1 January 2023. `[T9]`
- **Rule 144 holding period** is six months for a reporting issuer (reporting for at least 90 days) and one year otherwise. Form 144 is needed only above 5,000 shares or $50,000 in three months. `[T2]`
- **Accredited investor net worth** excludes the primary residence ("The person's primary residence shall not be included as an asset"). Income test: $200,000 individual or $300,000 joint "in each of the two most recent years". `[T1]`
- **Rule 506(b) counts only non-accredited purchasers** towards 35: Rule 501(e) excludes "Any accredited investor". Rule 506(c) requires all purchasers to be accredited. `[T2]`
- **SIPC is not FDIC.** SIPC advances up to $500,000 per customer with a $250,000 cash limit (15 U.S.C. 78fff-3). FDIC's SMDIA is $250,000 (12 CFR 330.1(o)). `[T1]`
- **SAR vs CTR.** SAR for broker-dealers: at least $5,000, filed within 30 calendar days (up to 60 if no suspect is identified). CTR: currency transactions of more than $10,000. `[T7]`
- **Money-laundering stages.** The outline lists "structuring, layering, placement". Structuring is a technique, not a classic stage. Teach what the source says and do not invent the third stage from memory. `[T7]`
- **Correspondence** is a communication to 25 or fewer retail investors within any 30-calendar-day period. Above that it is a retail communication (FINRA 2210). `[T7]`
- **Reg S-P** now includes customer notice of unauthorised access "not later than 30 days" after becoming aware (17 CFR 248.30). Older material lacks this; compliance dates not verified. `[T7]`
- **IRA limit changed for 2026:** $7,500 ($8,600 at 50+). 2025 and 2024: $7,000 ($8,000). RMDs start at age 73. `[T7]`
- **Specified adult** (FINRA 2165) is 65 and older, or 18 and older with an impairment. It is not the same as a trusted contact person (Rule 4512). `[T8]`
- **Suitability has three obligations** (reasonable-basis, customer-specific, quantitative). Reg BI has four (disclosure, care, conflict of interest, compliance). Candidates mix them up. `[T7]`
- **ICA diversified test** is 75/5/10: at least 75% of assets, no more than 5% in one issuer, no more than 10% of an issuer's voting securities (15 U.S.C. 80a-5). `[T4]`
- **Outline typo:** "Investment Adviser Act"; the statute is the Investment Advisers Act of 1940. `[T1]`

## Languages

English-only delivery is **not verified**. The outline and rules exist in

FINRA rules was found.
