# Scrum Product Owner: notes for every writer (read after DIGEST.md)

- **Slug:** `scrum-pspo`. **Family prefix:** `pspo` (card ids `pspo.<topic-slug>.<card-slug>`, concept ids `pspo.<concept-slug>`). Use the concept ids in `scrum-pspo-concepts.json`; add new ones only when a card needs them.
- **Builds on:** `scrum-guide`. Their registry terms (topic 0) count as taught: never re-introduce them.
- **Sources, their tiers and licence labels.** The label must match src/licences.json for the host. Tier A or B: "B · <licence>", with evidence. Tier C: "C · facts only, in our own words", with no evidence.
  - scrumguides.org → tier B ("© 2020 Ken Schwaber and Jeff Sutherland This publication is offered for license)
  - scrumorg-website-prod.s3.amazonaws.com → tier B ("© 2024 Scrum.org This publication is offered for license under the Attribution )
  - www.scrum.org → tier C ("2026 © Scrum.org. All Rights Reserved." (page footer); Scrum.org TM and © Guide)
- **Evidence is required.** The texts are in `research/sources/scrum-pspo/`; the slice prints a passage under each concept.
- **Volatile facts:** `volatile: true`, `validAsOf: "2026-09-25 · Scrum Guide 2020, EBM Guide 2024"`.
- **Scenarios:** your own. Never reuse a source's worked examples or exercises.
- **Deck notice:** already in deck.json.
