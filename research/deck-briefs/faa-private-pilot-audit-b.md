# faa-private-pilot audit, part b (topics 5 to 7)

Cards checked: 397 (05 airspace and VFR rules 131; 06 navigation 111; 07 aerodynamics 155). All 91.155 and 91.157 minimums, 91.215/91.225 equipment rules and every navigation, load-factor and stall-speed calculation were worked through step by step; the remaining arithmetic checks out.

ppl.navigation.heading-chain-apply | wrong | choicesExplained says option B (116°) comes from subtracting the WCA and the west variation. That would give 108° ± 2. 116° comes from adding the WCA but subtracting the west variation (125 − 7 − 2). | PHAK ch. 16 p. 9: "TH ± variation (V) = magnetic heading (MH) ± deviation (D) = compass heading (CH)"; p. 8: "if variation is west, add; if east, subtract"
