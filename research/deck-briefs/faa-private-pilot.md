## Traps

- [T1] **Time allowed:** the ACS-6C table says 2.5 hours; the Matrix (25 Sept 2026) says 2.0; the PSI CIB says 120 minutes. Teach 2.0 h, cite the Matrix.
- [T1] **BasicMed limits:** cards must use the current text. §61.113(i)(1) reads "not more than 7 occupants, has a maximum takeoff weight of not more than 12,500 pounds, is operated with no more than 6 passengers on board".
- [T1] Medical duration depends on age **at the exam**: third-class for private privileges ends with the 60th month (under 40) or 24th month (40 or older) after the exam month (§61.23(d)).
- [T1] Day currency (§61.57(a)) is any three takeoffs and landings (full stop only in a tailwheel); night currency (§61.57(b)) is three to a full stop between 1 hour after sunset and 1 hour before sunrise. Three different "nights" exist: lights (sunset to sunrise, §91.209), currency (1 hour after sunset), logging (§1.1 definition, taught in T0).
- [T1] The flight review is "since the beginning of the 24th calendar month before the month" (§61.56(c)): calendar-month counting, not 730 days.
- [T1] Pro rata: a private pilot "may not pay less than the pro rata share" of fuel, oil, airport expenditures or rental fees (§61.113(c)).
- [T1] Pilot certificates issued after 1 April 2016, including student certificates, have no expiry date (§61.19(c)); the paper student certificates that expired are the old ones.
- [T1] Sport pilot rules moved with MOSAIC (rule effective 22 Oct 2025): the PHAK addendum removes the §1.1 light-sport definition and changes sport-pilot privileges. PHAK-25C pages 1-15 to 1-17 are out of date until revised.
- [T2] Annual (12 calendar months) versus 100-hour (only for hire or instruction in an aircraft the instructor provides); the 100-hour limit may be exceeded by up to 10 hours to reach an inspection place (§91.409).
- [T2] ELT battery: replace after more than 1 cumulative hour of use or after 50 percent of its useful life (§91.207(c)); ELT inspection every 12 calendar months (§91.207(d)). Transponder check every 24 calendar months (§91.413).
- [T2] Inoperative equipment without an MEL: removed or deactivated **and** placarded (§91.213(d)); the decision is the pilot's.
- [T5] VFR minimums: Class B is "3 statute miles, Clear of Clouds"; Class G at or below 1,200 ft AGL by day is 1 SM clear of clouds; the 5 SM / 1,000-1,000-1 SM line applies at or above 10,000 MSL (and above 1,200 AGL in G) (§91.155). Candidates mix up B with C/D.
- [T5] Class C and D need two-way radio communication **established**, not a clearance; Class B needs a clearance (§§91.129–91.131).
- [T5] VFR cruising altitudes apply only "more than 3,000 feet above the surface" and use **magnetic course** (§91.159).
- [T5] Oxygen: crew above 12,500 to 14,000 ft cabin altitude for more than 30 minutes; crew at all times above 14,000; each occupant must be provided oxygen above 15,000 (§91.211).
- [T5] Fuel reserve is to the **first point of intended landing** plus 30 min by day, 45 min at night (§91.151).
- [T7] Stall speed rises with load factor: "unaccelerated stalling speed of 50 knots can be stalled at 100 knots by inducing a load factor of 4 Gs" (PHAK ch.5).
- [T9] Carburettor ice: the first sign with a fixed-pitch propeller is a drop in RPM; with a constant-speed propeller, "a decrease in manifold pressure, but no reduction in rpm" (PHAK ch.7). Full carb heat "will further reduce power and may cause engine roughness as melted ice goes through the engine".
- [T9] Detonation versus preignition are distinct; both are tested as a contrast.
- [T10] Blocked pitot with drain hole open vs closed, and blocked static, give different airspeed/altimeter/VSI symptoms (PHAK ch.8).
- [T12] Light signals mean different things on the ground and in the air (§91.125).
- [T14] NTSB: an accident report within 10 days; an overdue aircraft after 7 days if still missing (49 CFR 830.15). "Serious injury" includes hospitalisation for more than 48 hours starting within 7 days (§830.2). The Part 107 safety-event report (10 days to the FAA, §107.9) is a different rule.
- [T0/T6] Outdated material: the 2018 supplement figures still appear on the test until supplements are withdrawn; embedded images already feature on the Private Pilot test (ATCA Sept 2026). Chart cards should use current Chart User's Guide symbols.
- [T0] Handbook editions: PHAK is 25C (2023) with a MOSAIC addendum (20 Oct 2025); AFH is 3C with a MOSAIC addendum (October 2025, per the FAA handbooks page).

## Languages

The test is in English. The ACS requires the FAA Aviation English Language
Standard: "An applicant must be able to communicate in English in a discernible
and understandable manner" (ACS #page=76), and §61.103(c) makes English an
eligibility requirement. No FAA or PSI page fetched offers PAR in another
language. The Part 107 research found no official or openly licensed translation
of the FAA handbooks or rules; this task found none either (not searched further).
