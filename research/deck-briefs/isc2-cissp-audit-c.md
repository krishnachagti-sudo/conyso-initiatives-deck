# isc2-cissp audit, part C (topics 13–18)

Cards checked: 327 (13: 36, 14: 112, 15: 21, 16: 67, 17: 39, 18: 52). Tier C sources (ITU-T X.200, RFC 1122) are not cached (manifest path null), so I fetched them to scratch to check the cards: every X.200 `#page=` is correct. Figure `media/pen-test-phases.svg` was compared with SP 800-115 Figure 5-1 (PDF p. 37). The figure has flow arrows from Planning and Attack into Reporting; the SVG instead links all three phases to a Reporting bar. §5.2.1 (p. 39: reporting "occurs simultaneously with the other three phases") supports this, and the credit cites both the figure and §5.2.1, so there is no finding. Topics 13, 15 and 18 had no findings.
