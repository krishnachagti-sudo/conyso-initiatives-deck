SUBJECT: Revising Guidance on Naturalization Civics Educational Requirement
Purpose
U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) is issuing policy guidance in the USCIS Policy
Manual to implement the 2020 Naturalization Civics Test with some modifications (“2025
Naturalization Civics Test”), which helps ensure proper assimilation of lawful immigrants into the
United States and promote a unified American identity and attachment to the Constitution, laws,
and founding principles of the United States. 1 This update also provides clarification on the
requirements an alien must demonstrate to sufficiently establish knowledge and understanding of
civics as required under section 312 of the Immigration and Nationality Act (INA).
Background
The naturalization process provides aliens with the opportunity to become fully vested members of
American society with important rights and responsibilities that all citizens should exercise and
respect. Understanding their rights as new citizens, understanding and appreciating the principles and
ideals of the U.S. Constitution, engaging with the government, and fully contributing to the
democratic process and integrating into American society, all rely on a basic understanding of the
English language and American system of government (civics).
Under INA 312, most aliens seeking to naturalize must demonstrate an understanding of the English
language including an ability to speak, read, and write words in ordinary usage (English language
requirements) 2 and demonstrate a knowledge and understanding of U.S. history and of the principles
and form of government in the United States (civics requirements). 3 Aliens have two opportunities to
pass the related English and civics tests prior to denial of their application for naturalization. 4

Consistent with the Notice of Implementation of Modified 2020 Naturalization Civics Test, 90 FR
45047, USCIS is now reimplementing the 2020 Naturalization Civics Test, 8 with limited
modifications (“2025 Naturalization Civics Test”), which better fulfills the statutory requirement that
an alien must demonstrate “a knowledge and understanding of the fundamentals of the history, and of
the principles and form of government, of the United States” as required by INA 312. Implementing
the 2025 Naturalization Civics Test is part of an ongoing effort USCIS is taking to restore integrity to
the naturalization process. In addition, this update affirms assimilation expectations through USCIS’
assessment of an alien’s knowledge of the civics requirements for naturalization.
This guidance, contained in Volume 12 of the Policy Manual, applies to applications for
naturalization filed on or after October 20, 2025. The guidance contained in the Policy Manual is
controlling and supersedes any related prior guidance.
Policy Highlights
•

Provides that USCIS will administer the 2025 Naturalization Civics Test to aliens who file
for naturalization on or after October 20, 2025.

•

Increases the general bank of civics test questions to 128 (from 100), the number of test
questions for the exam to 20 (from 10), and accordingly, the number of correct answers
needed to pass the civics test to 12 (from six).

•
