
 § 1008.105 Minimum loan originator license requirements.
 This version is the current regulation
 Search this regulation
 Regulation H
{#247b3a64e77f3fe6378b6e9b74ffbe412c10f3e74d0af853027e1433} For an individual to be eligible for a loan originator license required under § 1008.103(a) and (d), a state must require and find, at a minimum, that an individual:
{#a} (a) Has never had a loan originator license revoked in any governmental jurisdiction, except that a formally vacated revocation shall not be deemed a revocation;
{#b} (b)
{#b-1} (1) Has never been convicted of, or pled guilty or **nolo contendere to, a felony in a domestic, foreign, or military court:
{#b-1-i} (i) During the 7-year period preceding the date of the application for licensing; or
{#b-1-ii} (ii) At any time preceding such date of application, if such felony involved an act of fraud, dishonesty, a breach of trust, or money laundering.
{#b-2} (2) For purposes of this paragraph (b):
{#b-2-i} (i) Expunged convictions and pardoned convictions do not, in themselves, affect the eligibility of the individual; and
{#b-2-ii} (ii) Whether a particular crime is classified as a felony is determined by the law of the jurisdiction in which an individual is convicted.
{#c} (c) Has demonstrated financial responsibility, character, and general fitness, such as to command the confidence of the community and to warrant a determination that the loan originator will operate honestly, fairly, and efficiently, under reasonable standards established by the individual state.
{#d} (d) Completed at least 20 hours of pre-licensing education that has been reviewed and approved by the Nationwide Mortgage Licensing System and Registry. The pre-licensing education completed by the individual must include at least:
{#d-1} (1) 3 hours of Federal law and regulations;
{#d-2} (2) 3 hours of ethics, which must include instruction on fraud, consumer protection, and fair lending issues; and
{#d-3} (3) 2 hours of training on lending standards for the nontraditional mortgage product marketplace.
{#e} (e)
{#e-1} (1) Achieved a test score of not less than 75 percent correct answers on a written test developed by the NMLSR in accordance with 12 U.S.C. 5105(d).
{#e-2} (2) To satisfy the requirement under paragraph (e)(1) of this section, an individual may take a test three consecutive times, with each retest occurring at least 30 days after the preceding test. If an individual fails three consecutive tests, the individual must wait at least 6 months before taking the test again.
{#e-3} (3) If a formerly state-licensed loan originator fails to maintain a valid license for 5 years or longer, not taking into account any time during which such individual is a registered loan originator, the individual must retake the test and achieve a test score of not less than 75 percent correct answers.
{#f} (f) Be covered by either a net worth or surety bond requirement, or pays into a state fund, as required by the state loan originator supervisory authority.
{#g} (g) Has submitted to the NMLSR fingerprints for submission to the Federal Bureau of Investigation and to any government agency for a state and national criminal history background check; and
{#h} (h) Has submitted to the NMLSR personal history and experience, which must include authorization for the NMLSR to obtain:
{#h-1} (1) Information related to any administrative, civil, or criminal findings by any governmental jurisdiction; and
{#h-2} (2) An independent credit report.
