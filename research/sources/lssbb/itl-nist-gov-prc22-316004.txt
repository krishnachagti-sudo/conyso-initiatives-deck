An illustrative example of the t-test

The following numbers are particle (contamination) counts for a sample
of 10 semiconductor silicon wafers:

50 48 44 56 61 52 53 55 67 51

The mean = 53.7 counts and the standard deviation = 6.567 counts.

The test is two-sided

Over a long run the process average for wafer particle counts has
been 50 counts per wafer, and on the basis of the sample, we want
to test whether a change has occurred. The null hypothesis that
the process mean is 50 counts is tested against the alternative
hypothesis that the process mean is not equal to 50 counts. The
purpose of the two-sided alternative is to rule out a possible
process change in either direction.
